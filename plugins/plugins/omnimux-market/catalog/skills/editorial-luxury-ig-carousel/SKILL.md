# Editorial Luxury IG Carousel

A five-slide Instagram carousel mold for premium brands. The aesthetic is magazine-cover gravitas: matte black, warm metallic gold, Didot/Bodoni serifs, restrained editorial layouts. The subject (brand, product, services) is always swappable — the visual system and the five-beat arc are not.

## When to use this pattern

**Use it for:**
- Premium creative studios, design agencies, photography/film studios, AI-art shops.
- Luxury verticals: fashion, jewelry, architecture, interiors, perfume, watches, hospitality.
- Personal-brand carousels for art directors, creative directors, cinematographers.
- Any "stop-scrolling" multi-slide IG post that needs *gravitas* over hype.

**Do NOT use it for:**
- Video ads, UGC, talking-head content, persona-driven product demos — those need the director/persona pipeline, not this skill.
- Mass-market / playful / hyped brands (gym wear, energy drinks, kids' toys). The whisper-not-shout register will read as cold.
- Single-image posts. The five-beat arc is the engine; one slide breaks it.
- Briefs where the user wants bright, colorful, maximalist, or pop-art aesthetics. The palette is non-negotiable.

## The creative move (one sentence)

Open with a provocative serif one-liner on matte black that names the target audience's status-quo pain, then walk through three numbered editorial "spreads" (one per service/offering, each its own visual world but sharing the same layout chrome), then land on a centered gold wordmark with a DM-keyword CTA. The unifying feeling is *editorial restraint* — like flipping through a luxury print magazine, not scrolling an ad.

## Hook & opening (Slide 1)

**Structural move:** Pattern-interrupt-via-restraint. Where every other carousel is loud, this one is *quiet and confident*. Bold serif typography, centered or left-aligned on matte black, ending with a small "swipe →" indicator in the bottom-right.

**Invented example (swap the subject for the brand's category):**
> "Everyone blends in. You weren't built to."
> *(swipe →)*

**Hook formulae (pick one, swap subject):**
1. *Status-quo callout:* "Most [category] look the same. Yours shouldn't."
2. *Quiet flex:* "Some brands market. Others get remembered."
3. *Negation:* "We don't make ads. We make brands worth watching."
4. *Question + tension:* "What if your brand looked like a film?"
5. *Two-line cadence:* "[Pain in line 1.] [Promise in line 2.]" Each line on its own typographic row.

**Rule:** Keep it ≤12 words. No emojis. No exclamation marks. The line breaks themselves are part of the design — set them deliberately, not by word-wrap.

## Narrative arc (5 beats, locked order)

| Slide | Beat | Function | What changes per-brand | What stays locked |
|-------|------|----------|------------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Hook** | Stop the scroll with a provocative serif one-liner. | The line itself. | Matte black, centered serif, "swipe →" mark. |
| 2 | **Service 01** | First flagship offering, shown as a cinematic still life. | Product/service noun, headline. | Top-left "01 — [SERVICE]" label, bottom-left serif headline. |
| 3 | **Service 02** | Second offering, rendered as a surreal/dreamlike scene (AI-aesthetic territory). | Subject of the dreamscape. | Same layout chrome as slide 2, with "02 — …". |
| 4 | **Service 03** | Third offering, rendered as a CGI/3D hero render or hyper-real product shot. | The hero object. | Same chrome, "03 — …". |
| 5 | **CTA** | Land the wordmark; tell viewer exactly what to do. | Brand name, tagline, DM keyword, service list. | Centered gold serif wordmark, tiny caps tagline, DM-keyword line, service list as small caps. |

If the brand has fewer than three services, repeat the strongest one across slides 2–4 with different visual treatments (still life → dreamscape → CGI) rather than padding with weak offerings. If it has more than three, pick the three that photograph best in this palette and leave the rest for the caption.

## Visual style spec (locked, do not regress)

- **Background:** Matte black across all five slides. Add a subtle film-grain / 35mm-grain texture — never clean digital black.
- **Accent color:** Warm metallic gold — think aged brass / champagne gold, not yellow gold and not pale gold. Use it for the brand wordmark, key emphasis words (1–2 per slide max), and the slide-1 "swipe →" arrow. Gold should always read as *gradient* metal, never flat.
- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: high-contrast serif in the Didot / Bodoni / Playfair family. Tight tracking. Mixed case for elegance; reserve ALL CAPS for tiny labels only.
  - Labels / numbers / tagline: clean white sans-serif (Helvetica/Inter/Söhne family), uppercase, generous letter-spacing.
  - Never mix more than these two families.
- **Aspect ratio:** 4:5 (Instagram portrait carousel). Render at 2K resolution minimum so the serif typography stays crisp on retina screens.
- **Camera grammar (for slides 2–4):**
  - Slide 2 (still life): medium-close, slight overhead or 3/4 angle, hard directional key light with one bounce. Think product-on-marble or product-on-velvet.
  - Slide 3 (dreamscape): wider, atmospheric haze, lens-flare permitted, surreal scale shifts (oversized objects, floating elements). This is the only slide where realism breaks.
  - Slide 4 (CGI): hyper-real 3D render aesthetic, clean studio lighting, single hero object centered or rule-of-thirds, shallow depth of field.
- **Shot length distribution (carousel = static, so read as *visual rhythm*):** typographic (slide 1) → tight (slide 2) → wide (slide 3) → tight (slide 4) → typographic (slide 5). The eye should breathe wide in the middle.
- **On-screen text policy:** Every slide except 3 carries text; slide 3 may carry just the "02 — …" label if the dreamscape is strong enough alone. Never let text exceed ~15% of the canvas area.

## Layout primitives (apply identically to slides 2, 3, 4)

- **Top-left:** small uppercase white sans-serif label, format `0N — SERVICE NAME` (em-dash, not hyphen). ~3–4% of canvas height.
- **Bottom-left:** white serif headline, 1–2 lines, left-aligned, sitting on a 6% bottom margin. Headline ≤8 words.
- **Bottom-right:** tiny brand mark (the wordmark at ~30% opacity, or a small monogram). Acts as a continuity signature.
- **Negative space:** the upper-right and middle-right quadrants stay empty. This is what makes it read as editorial rather than as an ad.

## Voice & persona

- **Tone:** calm-authoritative, slightly conspiratorial, never hyped. The brand knows it's good and doesn't need to shout.
- **Persona archetype:** the *quiet expert* / *art director* — speaks in fragments, trusts the reader.
- **Rhythm:** prefer short declarative sentences. Two-beat cadences ("We don't make ads. We make brands."). No questions to the camera. No "you" too often — let the imagery do the addressing.
- **Banned register:** hype words ("amazing," "incredible," "game-changing"), emojis, exclamation marks, hashtag-stuffing inside slide copy. Hashtags only live in the caption, not on the canvas.
- **Sample headline phrasings to riff on:**
  - "Identity, refined."
  - "Cinema, for brands."
  - "Built in pixels. Felt like film."
  - "Crafted, not generated." *(use sparingly if AI is part of the service stack — owns the tension)*

## CTA mechanic (Slide 5)

- **Centered composition.** Wordmark in warm-gold serif, large but not screen-filling (~30% of canvas height).
- **Directly below wordmark:** one-line tagline in small caps white sans-serif. ≤6 words.
- **Below tagline:** the DM-keyword CTA, format `DM "[KEYWORD]" to start.` The keyword should be a single word in the brand's vocabulary (e.g. `"VISION"`, `"CRAFT"`, `"STUDIO"`). Soft, not screamed.
- **Bottom of canvas:** the service list as small-caps white sans-serif, single line, em-dash separated. `BRANDING — AI ADS — CGI — EDITS`.

**Rule:** No link-in-bio language on slide 5. No "follow for more." No "tag a friend." The DM-keyword move is the only CTA — it filters for serious inquiries and matches the restrained register.

## Caption (drafted alongside the carousel)

Keep the caption in the same voice. Structure:
1. Lead with a one-line restatement of the hook (different wording from slide 1).
2. One paragraph (2–3 sentences) on positioning.
3. Service list.
4. DM keyword instruction (mirrors slide 5).
5. 4–6 hashtags, all lowercase, niche-specific (not `#instagood`).

## Hard rules / do-not-regress

1. **Matte black backgrounds only.** No white, no cream, no colored grounds. Ever.
2. **Two type families maximum** — one Didot/Bodoni serif, one clean sans-serif. No script fonts, no display novelties.
3. **Warm gold, never yellow gold, never silver, never rose gold.** Gold appears as a gradient, never flat.
4. **5 slides, in the exact order: hook → 01 → 02 → 03 → CTA.** Do not reorder. Do not extend to 6+ unless the user explicitly asks; if they do, insert the extra slide as `04 — …` before the CTA, using the same chrome.
5. **4:5 aspect ratio, 2K minimum resolution.** Square (1:1) breaks the editorial feel.
6. **No people's faces on slides 1–4 unless the brief is explicitly portrait/fashion.** Faces pull attention away from typography and break the artifact-as-magazine illusion.
7. **No emojis, no exclamation marks, no all-caps headlines.** Small-caps labels are the only place uppercase is allowed.
8. **Fire all 5 image generations in parallel** — they share a design system, not a dependency chain, so there is no reason to serialize.
9. **Default to gpt-image-2.5-sunburst for slides with heavy typography (1, 2, 4, 5).** It renders crisp serif letterforms more reliably than nano-banana-2.
10. **If gpt-image-2.5-sunburst content-moderation rejects a prompt** (common triggers: "model in metallic gown," named living artists, anything that reads as a real person), strip body-language language and artist names, then retry on nano-banana-2 as the fallback. Do not abandon the slide.
11. **Skip the structured director / persona / image-QA pipeline.** These are standalone editorial stills, not ad scenes. Call `generate_image` directly, in parallel.
12. **Skip AskUserQuestion when the brief is already concrete** (brand name + vibe + format are present). Use the brand description verbatim as creative direction. Premium briefs lose energy when interrogated.
13. **Brand subject is the only true variable.** Palette, typography, layout chrome, beat order, and CTA mechanic are locked. If the user pushes for "more color" or "more energy," that's a different skill — push back or hand off, do not dilute this one.

## Quick checklist before shipping

- [ ] Five slides, 4:5, 2K, matte-black grounds.
- [ ] Slide 1 hook ≤12 words, ends with a swipe indicator.
- [ ] Slides 2–4 each carry `0N — SERVICE` top-left and a serif headline bottom-left.
- [ ] Slide 5 has centered gold wordmark + tagline + DM-keyword CTA + service list.
- [ ] No emojis, no exclamation marks, no off-palette colors anywhere.
- [ ] Caption drafted in matching voice with DM-keyword mirroring slide 5.
