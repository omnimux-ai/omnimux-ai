# Persona Prompt Construction Guide

## Core template

Use this to build a new character persona for any new series. Swap bracketed values; keep the structural phrases that lock consistency.

```
A young [man/woman] in their early 20s with [skin descriptor, e.g. pale cool-toned fair / warm brown / deep ebony] skin, sharp angular jawline, slim lean build, [hair: color + length + texture, e.g. dark messy medium-length hair] falling across the forehead in an undone way, [eye color] deep-set eyes that carry a weight of sadness, thin face with defined cheekbones, high model facial features, symmetrical features, well-proportioned figure, natural skin texture.

[Distinctive detail sentence — e.g.: A small faint scar sits just below [their] left eye — Distinctive detail (visible in frame): a small faint scar beneath the left eye, clearly visible in every frame.]

[They wear / He wears] dark fantasy clothing — [garment 1: silhouette + material + color], [garment 2], [garment 3], all in [palette: deep charcoal / obsidian black / slate tones / etc.].

[They stand / He stands] in a cinematic dark fantasy city at night — ancient stone architecture, glowing ethereal [world-specific particle: star particles / ember ash / crystal shards] floating in the cold air, dramatic moody shadows, deep indigo and black sky with faint silver [light: starlight / moonlight / bioluminescent glow] filtering through.
```

## Style direction (use verbatim or adapt)

```
Cinematic fantasy anime aesthetic, anamorphic 85mm shallow depth of field, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting with deep shadows and selective rim light, cool indigo and dark slate color palette, melancholic contemplative mood, epic movie quality, ultra detailed. No warm golden tones, no bright colors, no cheerful atmosphere.
```

## Episode narration line checklist

Before committing any narration line:
- [ ] Word count: 8–13 words
- [ ] Sentence is a single declarative or question — no compound clauses
- [ ] Does not resolve the episode's tension
- [ ] Uses literary register (not conversational)
- [ ] Target language checked for naturalness

## Episode structure at a glance

| Scene | Purpose | Shot type |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | World rule stated | Wide establishing |
| 2 | World texture deepened | Medium environmental |
| 3 | Protagonist first appear | Medium-close |
| 4 | Protagonist POV reaction | Close-up face |
| 5–7 | Inciting tension | Varied; at least one over-shoulder |
| 8–10 | Rising dread | Close-ups + one wide for scale |
| 11 | Near-revelation | Extreme close-up or symbolic wide |
| 12 | Cliffhanger freeze | Hold on face or symbol; no movement |
