# Visual Archetypes — Paper-Cutout Explainer

A library of proven scene compositions, indexed by their narrative function. Pick one archetype per beat. The subject inside each archetype is swappable; the layout, palette, and motion rules are not.

---

## Beat 1 — Anchor / origin

### Archetype A: Horizontal paper timeline
Top-down kraft-paper desk. A thin dusty-navy horizontal line runs across the middle of the frame as a timeline. A small dusty-navy paper cut-out of the *origin object* (early artifact, first seed, first coin, first telephone — whichever fits the topic) pops in on the left in 12fps stutter, with a tiny serif paper tag below it stating the year or origin phrase in quotes. One or two faint future tick-marks on the timeline to the right hint at progression. Empty cream space dominates — the origin must feel singular and small.

Coral accent: a single hand-drawn coral asterisk above the origin object at ~5s.

### Archetype B: Single labelled artifact
Centered single paper cut-out of the topic's foundational artifact, surrounded by cream space. Small cut-newsprint serif label below names it. Two or three hand-drawn black-ink arrows point inward from the edges, each with a one-word annotation (e.g. "fragile", "early", "manual"). Coral underline on the central label stamps in at 5s.

---

## Beat 2 — Scale / shift

### Archetype A: Paper bar chart erupting upward
Top-down. Five to seven dusty-navy paper rectangles stand vertically along a baseline at the bottom third of the frame, growing in height left to right. Each pops in in 12fps stutter, in sequence. A hand-drawn coral trend line stamps diagonally across the tops of the bars at ~4.5s. A cut-newsprint banner stamps in at the top right at 5s with a verbatim quoted phrase like `"EXPONENTIAL"` or `"10× IN 5 YEARS"`.

### Archetype B: Before / after split
Two paper rectangles side by side. Left: a dusty-navy paper cut-out representing the old state, with a small label like `"8 hrs"`. Right: a much smaller / faster / leaner paper cut-out representing the new state, with label `"8 mins"`. A coral hand-drawn arrow sweeps from left to right at 5s with a coral "→" stamp.

## Beat 3 — Spread / application

### Archetype A: Paper world map with coral nodes
Top-down. A simplified dusty-navy paper cut-out world map fills the frame, continents as flat hand-scissored shapes with visible jagged edges. Small coral circular paper dots pop in at major regions in 12fps stutter. Hand-drawn black-ink dashed arc connectors appear between dots in sequence. One coral node pulses faintly at the climax. Tiny serif paper tags label two or three of the nodes with place names in quotes.

### Archetype B: Paper device with pop-out cards
Top-down. A dusty-navy paper cut-out device sits centered on cream paper. Three or four small paper cards pop out from its edges in 12fps stutter, each with a cut-newsprint serif label naming a use case in quotes (e.g. `"STEP ONE"`, `"STEP TWO"`, `"STEP THREE"`). One card gets a coral underline at the climax.

### Archetype C: Paper X-ray / scan
Top-down. A dusty-navy paper cut-out of the target object (X-ray, leaf, document, brain scan). A coral horizontal scan beam sweeps top-to-bottom across it. At ~5s a coral circular detection ring locks onto a specific point inside the object, with a small serif paper tag pointing to it in quotes (e.g. `"DETECTED"`).

## Beat 4 — Implication / future

### Archetype A: Paper neural mesh
Top-down. A web of dusty-navy paper nodes and thin black-ink lines fills the frame, growing outward from the center in 12fps stutter. A single coral node pulses at the center at the climax with a coral hand-drawn asterisk beside it. A cut-newsprint banner stamps in at the bottom with a verbatim quoted phrase like `"WHAT'S NEXT"` or `"THE NEXT DECADE"`.

### Archetype B: Row of paper figures with icons
Top-down. Four small dusty-navy paper cut-out human silhouettes stand in a row across the middle of the frame. Above each, a small paper icon pops in in 12fps stutter representing a different role / capability. Each figure has a tiny serif paper tag below stating their role in quotes (e.g. `"STUDENT"`, `"WORKER"`, `"PARENT"`, `"RETIREE"`). The icons above one figure get a coral asterisk at the climax.

### Archetype C: Paper centerpiece with sprouting branches
Top-down. A central dusty-navy paper cut-out hero shape (a brain, a tree, a hub — whatever fits the topic). Thin black-ink branches sprout outward, each ending in a small paper icon representing a future application. A coral underline stamps under the centerpiece at the climax with a verbatim phrase in quotes.

---

## Universal rules across all archetypes

- Background is always cream paper with very faint halftone dot pattern and a few scattered tiny hand-drawn marker asterisks.
- Every paper element has a visible soft drop shadow — layers must look like they float above each other.
- Edges are hand-scissored jagged, never clean vector.
- Exactly one coral-red `#E64A3E` accent moment per scene, landing at ~4.5–5s.
- All text labels are quoted verbatim in the motion prompt and described as "locked, static paper elements, no morphing, no animation."
