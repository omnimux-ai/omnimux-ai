# Cinematic Thriller Book — Multi-Format Campaign

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Ideal for:**
- Crime, thriller, mystery, horror, paranormal fiction — any genre where dread, tension, or a curse/gift duality is the emotional engine.
- Authors selling directly from their own storefront (not relying on Kindle Unlimited / Amazon exclusivity).
- Dual-format products (ebook + audiobook) where the price-savings angle is a real conversion lever.
- Platforms: Meta (Feed 1:1 image, Reels 9:16 vertical video), TikTok, YouTube Shorts.

**Not suited for:**
- Lighthearted romance, children's books, or non-fiction self-help — the atmospheric dread aesthetic is a mismatch.
- Campaigns that need a comedic or UGC-casual voice — this pattern is polished, cinematic, and narrator-led.

---

## 2. Hook & Opening

**Hook archetype: Philosophical-question / gift-curse duality**

The structural move is to open with a tension-framing question that makes the audience wonder whether the protagonist's special ability is salvation or damnation. The question hangs unanswered for 2–3 seconds before the title/product is revealed.

**Invented generic example (for a neutral hypothetical thriller):**
> "What if the only person who can hear the dead is the one they're hunting?"

**Swap rule:** Replace the gift-curse frame with the specific ability or situation of the new book's protagonist. Keep the two-sided, irresolvable tension. The question should be answerable two ways simultaneously.

**Three hook angles to always produce (one per ad unit):**

| Angle | Move | Visual treatment |
|---|---|---|
| A — Philosophical question | Gift/curse duality or "what if" impossible choice | Book cover hero on dark gradient background; question text large center |
| B — Environmental tension | Protagonist or environment establishes dread before title appears | Split composition: atmospheric environment (forest, city at night, etc.) + book cover emerging from shadow |
| C — Premium promo | Social proof + format badges + savings call-out | Clean, high-contrast layout; ebook/audiobook badges; "Save up to X% off" |

For video ads, use three hook angles that map to the three duration tiers (long ≈21s, medium ≈18s, short ≈15s). The shortest ad should be the most punchy and direct.

## 3. Narrative Arc

### Image ads
Single beat — everything communicates in one frame. Hook copy, atmospheric imagery, and product identity must be legible at a glance.

### Video ads — beat structure

**Long-form (≈21s): Slow dread build**

| Beat | Duration | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Hook question (VO + on-screen) | 0–4s | Quiet, conspiratorial — barely above a whisper |
| World-building / atmosphere | 4–14s | Cinematic b-roll, no dialogue, music swells subtly |
| Protagonist / series tease | 14–18s | VO delivers 1-sentence series premise |
| CTA — book cover reveal + spoken offer | 18–21s | Authoritative, direct; music drops to near-silence |

**Medium-form (≈18s): Tension-then-reveal**

| Beat | Duration | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Provocative hook VO | 0–3s | Deadpan authority |
| Atmospheric scene escalation | 3–13s | Visual tension, VO continues premise |
| CTA — cover + spoken offer | 13–18s | Clear, warm, direct |

**Short-form (≈15s): Immediate punch**

| Beat | Duration | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Bold hook statement (not question) | 0–2s | Sharp, no breath |
| Rapid premise delivery | 2–11s | Fast cuts, VO is urgent |
| CTA | 11–15s | Cover, offer, done |

## 4. Visual Style Spec

**Overall aesthetic:** Cinematic premium — not UGC, not lo-fi. The audience should feel like they're watching a prestige cable trailer.

**Color palette:**
- Deep blacks and charcoal backgrounds.
- Single accent color pulled from the book cover art (e.g., deep forest green, blood crimson, electric blue).
- Text in white or near-white. No warm tones unless the cover demands it.

**Camera grammar (video scenes):**
- Atmospheric scenes: slow push-in or locked-off wide shot. No handheld shaking.
- Close-up details: hands, eyes, objects — held for tension, not cut quickly.
- CTA scene: static, product-centered. Book cover is the hero. Background is muted (desaturated, dark, or blurred version of the atmospheric imagery).

**Shot length distribution:**
- Long-form: 3–5 second average shot, no cut under 2s.
- Short-form: 1.5–2.5 second average, fastest cut at 1s.

**On-screen text policy:**
- Hook text: large, centered, serif or bold sans — appears with VO and lingers 1s after VO finishes.
- Captions (video): `preset=glow`, `font_family=Alice` — literary thriller feel, subtle glow that reads on dark backgrounds without a hard box.
- Hook overlay styles: `punch` for urgency/thriller energy; `outlined` for paranoia/dread angles.
- CTA scene: text overlay on a muted/desaturated final scene background. Cover art renders at full saturation over the muted bg.
- No lower-thirds. No busy motion graphics. Restraint = prestige.

**Music:**
- Dark, suspenseful instrumental — crime thriller / neo-noir genre.
- Duration matched to longest video ad; shorter ads use a trimmed version from the same track for audio consistency across the campaign.
- `music_volume: 0.12` — subtle underscore beneath narrator VO. Music should never compete with the voice.

## 5. Voice & Persona

**Persona archetype:** Calm-authoritative narrator — female-presenting, middle-aged or ageless, measured pacing. Sounds like someone who knows how the story ends and is choosing what to reveal.

**Tone:** Not hype. Not whispery ASMR. Sits between "documentary narrator" and "trusted librarian who has seen things." Slightly conspiratorial — like she's letting you in on something.

**Speech pacing:** Slow at the hook (let the question breathe), moderate through premise, clear and slightly warmer at the CTA.

**Sample phrasing rhythm:**
- Hook: "What if [X] was also [Y]?" — pause — let silence work.
- Premise: One or two sentences maximum. Name the protagonist, name the ability or situation, name the cost.
- CTA: "Get [series name] [format descriptor] directly from the author's bookstore and save up to [X]% off." — spoken without urgency, as if it's obvious.

**Pronunciation note:** If the protagonist's name or the author's name has a non-standard pronunciation, add a parenthetical pronunciation note inline in every VO script, e.g., `[Name] (pronounced "[phonetic spelling]")`. Do not leave pronunciation ambiguous and assume the voice generator will guess correctly.

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**CTA type:** Direct-purchase / price-savings + format callout.

**Spoken CTA wording pattern:**
> "Get [Title]: [Series Name] as an ebook or audiobook directly from the author's bookstore and save up to [X]% off."

**Visual CTA treatment:**
- Final scene of every video ad: book cover art rendered at full color/saturation, centered or slightly right-of-center.
- Background: desaturated or blurred version of the prior atmospheric scene — creates continuity without distraction.
- On-screen text overlay (separate from captions): author website or "author's bookstore" callout.
- No spoken platform names (e.g., no mention of Kindle, Amazon, Audible). The author's store is the only destination.

**Image ad CTA (Angle C — premium promo):**
- Ebook badge + audiobook badge displayed prominently.
- "Save up to X% off" in a callout element.
- Author website URL or handle.

**What the CTA must NOT include:**
- Kindle Unlimited references.
- Any third-party retail platform names.
- Urgency language ("limited time", "sale ends soon") unless explicitly approved by the client.

---

## 7. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

Treat each of the following as a binding constraint for all future runs of this skill.

1. **No Kindle Unlimited or retail platform references.** The CTA copy must never mention KU, Kindle, Audible, or any third-party retail platform. The author sells direct; the offer is savings vs. retail — not a subscription pitch.

2. **Pronunciation must be specified for unusual names.** Add `(pronounced "X")` inline in every VO script for any name that could be mispronounced. Do not leave pronunciation ambiguous.

3. **Book cover is PRODUCT REFERENCE in CTA scenes only.** In atmospheric scenes (beats 1–3 of video), the cover art is absent or appears only as a subtle background element. It is the hero in the final CTA beat. Do not display it throughout every scene — that kills the cinematic feel.

4. **Music volume stays at 0.12.** The narrator VO is the primary audio signal. Music is texture, not feature. Do not raise above 0.15 without explicit client approval.

5. **Caption style locked: `preset=glow`, `font_family=Alice`.** This combination encodes "literary thriller" in the typography. Do not switch to default captions or bold-box captions.

6. **Hook overlay styles: `punch` (urgency angles) and `outlined` (paranoid/dread angles).** Match the hook mood to the overlay style. Do not use the same style for all three video ads.

7. **Three video ad durations: long / medium / short (≈21s / 18s / 15s).** Always produce a duration ladder. Each ad must have a different hook angle — not just a trimmed version of the longest.

8. **Three image ad hook angles (A, B, C).** Always produce all three angles. Do not collapse them into one generic image ad.

9. **9:16 aspect ratio for all video; 1:1 for all image ads** (Meta Feed). Adjust if platform target changes, but maintain the distinction between formats.

10. **No warm color grading on atmospheric scenes** unless the cover art palette explicitly demands it. The default is cool, desaturated, dark.
