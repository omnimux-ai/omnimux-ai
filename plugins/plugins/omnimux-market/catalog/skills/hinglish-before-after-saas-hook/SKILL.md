# Hinglish Before-vs-After SaaS Hook

A vertical video creative pattern for Indian B2B/SaaS products. The mold: **relatable late-night struggle → PEHLE vs AB card → product reveal → Hinglish CTA**. The subject (product, profession, pain point) is always swappable. The structure, language register, visual grammar, and emotional arc stay locked.

---

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Fits:**
- Indian urban professional audiences (CAs, tax consultants, lawyers, HR managers, startup founders, clinic owners)
- SaaS / productivity / fintech / legaltech products with a clear manual-vs-automated contrast
- Platforms: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok (9:16 vertical, 15–45s)
- Briefs where the user has uploaded product UI screenshots (portal, dashboard, mobile app)

**Does not fit:**
- Consumer lifestyle products without a "work pain" angle
- English-only brands targeting Tier 1 global markets
- Ads longer than 60s (the pacing breaks down)
- Products where the "before" state isn't visually demonstrable

---

## 2. Hook & Opening

**Structural move:** POV question + time-anchored struggle. Open with the persona speaking directly to camera in Hinglish, naming the exact painful moment ("raat ke 2 baje…"). This is a **pattern interrupt** — the viewer recognises themselves instantly.

**Invented example (neutral hypothetical productivity tool):**
> *"Kya aap bhi raat bhar reports manually banate ho? 2 baje raat ko, ek-ek number, formatting… yaar, kab tak?"*

**Swap rule:** Replace the time anchor, the task name, and the quantified pain with your product's equivalent. Keep the Hinglish sentence structure, the direct-to-camera address, and the trailing "kab tak?" / "kab tak karenge?" rhetorical close.

**Hook overlay (on-screen text):** A single punchy Hindi/Hinglish question in large bold Montserrat (punch style), white text + dark semi-transparent backing, overlaid on the first 2–3 seconds. Example: *"Kya Aap Bhi Raat Bhar Reports Banate Ho?"*

---

## 3. Narrative Arc

Total target: **30 seconds** (can compress to 20s or extend to 45s by trimming/extending beats 2–4).

| Beat | Duration | What happens | Voice/visual tone |
|------|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| **1 — Struggle hook** | 0–8s | Persona on camera, direct address, names the painful manual task. Shot: medium close-up, handheld, tungsten desk lamp, late night. | Tired, conspiratorial. Speaks like a peer venting, not an actor performing. |
| **2 — Pain amplification** | 8–13s | Action shot or VO over visual chaos — stacked papers, cluttered screen, multiple browser tabs. No talking head; show the mess. | VO continues. Tense underscore music. |
| **3 — VS card** | 13–16s | Static graphic: "PEHLE vs AB" or "BEFORE vs AFTER" split. Dark/muted left panel (struggle), bright/clean right panel (product). Bold white sans-serif. | Music cuts momentarily or drops to near-silence for 0.5s — then upbeat kicks in. |
| **4 — Product reveal** | 16–23s | Persona relaxed, daytime or brighter light, product on laptop/phone. On camera: "Ab sirf ek click mein ho jaata hai." Screen demo inset or Ken Burns zoom into UI. | Calm, satisfied, slightly impressed. Upbeat Indian corporate/tech beat. |
| **5 — CTA** | 23–30s | Branded end card (brand color background, logo, CTA text). Persona VO soft sign-off: "Try karo aaj, link bio mein hai." On-screen: pill button with brand color accent. | Warm, friendly, not pushy. |

---

## 4. Visual Style Spec

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920
- **Cinematography:** Documentary/UGC-adjacent handheld for struggle beats; slightly more stable (but still not tripod-locked) for reveal beats
- **Before scenes:** Tungsten desk lamp as key light, cool ambient fill. Color grade: desaturated, slightly blue-tinted shadows. Cluttered background.
- **After scenes:** Brighter, neutral-warm daylight or diffused window light. Cleaner background. Product screen clearly visible.
- **Shot length distribution:** Hook beat — one medium/long take (5–8s). Pain beat — faster cuts (2–3s each). VS card — static hold (2–3s). Reveal — medium takes (3–4s) with one Ken Burns product screen zoom.
- **On-screen text:** Hinglish captions in Montserrat or similar geometric sans. Deep blue or white. Subtitles for all spoken VO (accessibility + muted viewers). Hook overlay uses large "punch" style; rest uses subtle lower-thirds.
- **Product UI integration:** If product screenshots are provided, use them as start-frame references in screen-demo scenes. Ken Burns slow zoom (1.0→1.08 scale over ~5s) keeps it dynamic without feeling slideshowy.
- **VS card:** Generate as a static image. Left panel dark/muted with "PEHLE" label; right panel bright/clean with "AB" label. Bold white display font. Separate from video pipeline; drop in as a scene.
- **CTA end card:** Brand primary color background. Logo centered or top. CTA line in white. Pill-shaped button accent in contrasting color (e.g. green on navy). Generate as static image.

---

## 5. Voice & Persona

**Persona archetype:** Young urban Indian professional — the peer, not the expert. 22–32 years old. Speaks from personal experience, not as a brand spokesperson.

**Tone:** Tired-but-relatable in the before beats → calm-satisfied in the after beats. Deadpan humor is welcome ("yaar, kab tak?" lands better than exasperation). Never hyped or salesy.

**Language register:** Hinglish — Hindi sentence structure with English nouns for technical terms. Ratio roughly 60% Hindi / 40% English. Avoid pure English sentences in VO; avoid formal Hindi.

**Sample phrasing patterns:**
- Hook: *"Kya aap bhi [pain task] karte ho? [time anchor] mein [quantified struggle]…"*
- Pain: *"Ek-ek [manual step], ghanton ki mehnat, aur phir bhi galti ho jaati hai."*
- Reveal: *"Ab [product name] se sirf [time] mein [outcome]."*
- CTA: *"Free try karo, link [bio/description] mein hai."*

**Persona brief elements to define for each new run:**
- Profession & age (e.g. "young Indian professional, 24, early career")
- Wardrobe signal of the "before" state (loosened tie, rolled-up sleeves = overworked)
- Setting (office desk, home office, co-working space)
- Time of day for before/after contrast (night vs day works best)

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**Type:** Soft spoken sign-off + on-screen pill button. Not aggressive comment-bait.

**Spoken:** *"[Product name] free try karo — link [bio/description] mein hai."* Delivered conversationally, not as an announcement.

**On-screen (end card):**
- Pill-shaped button with contrasting accent color
- Text: e.g. "Try [Product] Free →" in English (CTAs perform better in English even in Hinglish ads)
- Brand logo visible
- No countdown timer; no "limited offer" language unless brief explicitly requires it

**Placement:** Last 5–7 seconds, full end card frame. Do not overlay CTA text on product demo footage — keep it clean.

---

## 7. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

Treat each as non-negotiable:

1. **Hinglish, not pure Hindi, not pure English.** The language register is the cultural signal. Do not default to English VO even if the brand name is English.
2. **VS card is a separate static image — not a director-rendered video scene.** Generate it with `generate_image` and drop it as a scene in assembly. Director-rendered VS cards lose the clean graphic punch.
3. **CTA end card is also a static image** (same reason). Generate separately; don't try to animate it.
4. **Music must transition.** Tense/minor underscore for struggle beats; upbeat Indian corporate/tech beat for reveal beats. Single flat music track kills the emotional contrast. Use a track that transitions, or layer two tracks with crossfade at the VS card cut.
5. **Music volume stays low (≤ 0.15).** VO intelligibility is paramount. Background music must not compete with speech.
6. **Product screenshots must be used as reference.** If the user uploads UI screenshots, classify each one and reference the most relevant one per product-demo scene. Do not invent UI; do not use generic stock mockups.
7. **Persona visual description must be locked before parallel director calls.** Capture exact appearance (hair, clothing, skin tone, background) from the persona image result and paste it verbatim into every director prompt. Consistency across scenes is non-negotiable.
8. **Hook overlay text goes on the first scene only** — not repeated across scenes. One bold question, one time.
9. **Caption style: deep blue Montserrat, not white-on-white.** White captions on bright frames vanish. Use deep blue or a dark semi-transparent backing.
10. **Do not cast the persona as a "spokesperson."** The brief is always peer-to-peer. The persona is another professional who found the solution, not a brand ambassador.
