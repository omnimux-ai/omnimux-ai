# Luxury Dark Carousel — 5-Slide Instagram Pattern

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Best fit:**
- Digital products (templates, e-books, planners, presets, courses, productivity kits)
- Info-product / creator-economy topics (passive income, side hustles, productivity)
- Personal brands targeting aspirational / premium-feeling Instagram audiences
- Any brief calling for "luxury", "premium", "dark aesthetic", "gold accents", or "high-end"

**Poor fit:**
- Bright, playful, or child-friendly brands
- Physical product e-commerce that needs lifestyle photography carousels
- Brands with strict existing light-background style guides

---

## 2. Hook & Opening (Slide 1 — Cover)

**Structural move:** *Aspiration-gate* — the cover presents the subject (person or product) bathed in a dramatic cinematic spotlight against a dark gradient, with a bold imperative headline that names a desirable transformation. The visual says "this is a high-value world you could enter"; the headline opens the gate.

**Invented example (neutral hypothetical product):**
> Headline: "BUILD YOUR FIRST ONLINE STORE"
> Subheadline: "Turn a simple idea into a shop that runs while you sleep."

**Swap rule:** Replace the imperative verb phrase and the noun ("ONLINE STORE") with whatever the new product/topic is. Keep the dark gradient background, the gold 3D ring halos around the subject, the sparkle doodle cluster in the upper-right quadrant, and the cinematic overhead spotlight. The subject image is always composited (not replaced by illustration).

**Visual grammar for Slide 1:**
- Subject centered or slightly lower-third, composited onto a dark radial gradient (`#1F332A → #0F0F0F`)
- Two–three gold 3D rings encircling or floating near the subject
- Cluster of hand-drawn gold sparkle/star doodles in upper-right
- Bold white uppercase headline, with one key word or phrase in metallic gold `#D4AF37`
- Ivory subheadline directly below, smaller weight
- Thin gold decorative divider line between headline and subheadline

## 3. Narrative Arc

| Slide | Beat | Name | Target feel | Key visual |
|-------|------|------|-------------|------------|
| 1 | Hook | **Cover** | Aspiration — "I want that" | Subject + cinematic spotlight |
| 2 | Why | **Benefit grid** | Credibility — "This makes sense" | 4 gold-icon benefit points, doodle arrows |
| 3 | What | **Product showcase** | Possibility — "I could do this" | 5 floating 3D product cards |
| 4 | How | **Workflow path** | Clarity — "It's achievable" | 3D station-to-station workflow |
| 5 | CTA | **Close** | Urgency + warmth | Subject + doodle frame + CTA pill |

**Pacing fingerprint:** Each slide should feel self-contained (a reader who stops at any slide gets value), but slides 2–4 together form a logical "why → what → how" triptych that rewards swiping through.

## 4. Visual Style Spec

### Color palette (locked — do not substitute)
| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Dark Sage Green | `#1F332A` | Primary bg gradient start, card backgrounds |
| Matte Black | `#0F0F0F` | Gradient end, deep shadows |
| Metallic Gold | `#D4AF37` | Icons, doodles, accents, CTA button, key words |
| Ivory | `#F5F0E8` | Subheadlines, body text, secondary labels |

### Camera & lighting grammar
- **Cinematic spotlight:** soft overhead key light from top-center, creates a cone of illumination on the subject or hero element; surrounding areas fall into dark shadow
- **Depth of field:** background elements are subtly blurred / soft; foreground doodles and 3D elements sharp
- **Soft grain film texture:** a barely-visible fine-grain overlay across every slide (gives analog warmth without noisiness)

### 3D floating elements (required on every slide)
- Gold 3D rings or orbs near the subject (Slide 1, 5)
- Product card 3D mockups with subtle drop shadows (Slide 3)
- Workflow station icons with 3D extrusion / metallic finish (Slide 4)
- Small gold geometric shapes (diamonds, spheres) as filler elements on Slides 2–4

### Hand-drawn gold doodle overlay (required on every slide)
Every slide must include at least 3 of these: sparkles (`✦ ✧ ✨`), outlined stars, curved arrows, loose circles/loops, hand-drawn checkmarks. Doodles are always in `#D4AF37`, appear hand-sketched (not computer-perfect), and are scattered across the negative space — never overlapping the main text.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** bold, uppercase, white (`#FFFFFF`), tight tracking; one key word/phrase swapped to gold
- **Subheadlines / body:** ivory (`#F5F0E8`), sentence case or short all-caps, lighter weight
- **Gold decorative dividers:** thin horizontal lines or small diamond separators between headline and sub-copy blocks
- **No serif fonts** — the aesthetic is clean modern sans, luxury-minimal

### Aspect ratio & resolution
- Always `4:5` (1080×1350 portrait), 2K resolution
- All 5 slides must share identical canvas dimensions

## 5. Slide-by-Slide Design Spec

### Slide 1 — Cover (uses subject image)
- Dark radial gradient bg (`#1F332A` center → `#0F0F0F` edges)
- Subject composited, centered/lower-centered, lit by spotlight
- 2–3 gold 3D rings floating around subject
- Sparkle doodle cluster upper-right
- Bold headline top-third, gold accent word
- Ivory subheadline, gold divider

### Slide 2 — Why (pure design)
- 4 benefit bullet points in a 2×2 or vertical stacked layout
- Each point: gold 3D icon left, short bold white label, 1-line ivory description
- Doodle arrows pointing inward toward each benefit cluster
- Background: matte black with subtle dark sage vignette
- Suggested benefits (swap for new product domain): Low startup cost · Sell worldwide · No inventory · Passive income potential

### Slide 3 — What (pure design)
- 5 floating 3D product card mockups in a slight arc or staggered grid
- Each card labeled in gold text (the product type names)
- Cards cast soft drop shadows; foreground cards overlap background cards (depth)
- Bold headline top, doodle scatter around cards
- Suggested product categories (swap): Design Templates · E-books · Productivity Templates · Digital Planners · Social Media Kits

### Slide 4 — How (pure design)
- Horizontal or diagonal workflow path with 3–4 stations
- Each station: 3D icon in gold, station label in white, connecting gold line/arrow
- Path has slight perspective depth (stations shrink slightly toward center-right)
- Bold headline top
- Suggested workflow (swap): Ideate → Design → Package → Sell

### Slide 5 — CTA (uses subject image)
- Subject centered, slightly smaller than Slide 1 to allow frame breathing room
- Hand-drawn doodle border framing the slide perimeter (loose gold lines/loops)
- Gold particle scatter in background
- Bold CTA headline, ivory subheadline
- CTA pill button: gold background `#D4AF37`, matte black text, rounded corners
- Suggested CTA copy (swap): "DOWNLOAD • DESIGN • SELL"

## 6. Voice & Persona

This carousel has **no spoken voice** — it's a static visual format. But the copywriting voice is:
- **Tone:** calm-authoritative with aspiration. Not hype. Not desperate. The brand knows its value.
- **Phrasing rhythm:** short imperative headlines (3–6 words), one-line subheadlines (8–12 words max), benefit points in 2–4 word fragments
- **Key word pattern:** the most important word or phrase in each headline gets gold color treatment — this is the "whisper accent" that draws the eye without shouting
- **CTA mechanic:** pill button with a triple-action phrase separated by bullets ("DOWNLOAD • DESIGN • SELL") — speaks to the outcome sequence, not a vague "click here". Always spoken as an action triad.

---

## 7. Subject Image Handling

- **Always require** the user to upload their subject image before generation begins. Check via `list_assets(kind=input)` — look for an image asset.
- Inspect the image via `get_asset` before writing prompts, to understand subject framing, skin tone, clothing color, and background — then adapt the spotlight and ring placement accordingly.
- Slides 1 and 5 use `image_urls=[<input-asset-id>]` to composite the subject. Slides 2, 3, 4 have no `image_urls`.
- **Never** use the subject image as the sole visual — it must always be composited into the designed dark-gradient environment.
- Generate all 5 slides in parallel (no sequential dependency between slides).

---

## 8. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

These are locked creative decisions that must not be relaxed:

1. **Doodles on every slide.** Every slide must have hand-drawn gold doodle overlays. Removing them for "clean" aesthetic defeats the pattern.
2. **3D floating elements on every slide.** Flat icon grids without 3D depth break the luxury feel.
3. **Cinematic glow / depth of field on every slide.** No flat uniform lighting.
4. **Dark background only.** Do not use white, light grey, or pastel backgrounds even if the product brand uses them — the carousel background is always dark sage or matte black.
5. **Metallic gold accents only.** Do not substitute yellow, orange-gold, or champagne tones. Hex `#D4AF37` is the reference.
6. **Slides 1 and 5 must composite the actual subject image.** Do not illustrate the person / product from scratch on those slides.
7. **All 5 slides share identical dimensions (4:5, 2K).** Never mix orientations within the set.
8. **Subject is always swappable.** Every other element — palette, doodles, 3D style, narrative arc, CTA mechanic — stays locked. The subject (person, product, brand) is the only variable.
