# Headline phrase library

Voice: flat declarative or imperative, 3–6 words, no punctuation (except a single comma at a clause break), no emoji, no questions. Rendered all-uppercase, broken onto two lines at a natural phrase boundary.

## Imperative shapes (verb-led, second-person implied)

- `TRAIN PAST\nYOUR LIMIT`
- `MOVE LIKE\nA PRO`
- `OWN THE\nWORKOUT`
- `PUSH EVERY\nREP`
- `BRING IT\nHOME`
- `RAISE YOUR\nGAME`

## Flat claim shapes (the product / experience IS X)

- `STRONGER FRAME\nFEWER PARTS`
- `ENGINEERED TO\nOUTLAST`
- `MADE FOR PROS,\nUSED BY ALL`
- `PEAK FORM\nDAILY`
- `EVERY REP\nCOUNTS`
- `PRO-GRADE,\nHOME-READY`

## Line-break rule of thumb

Pick the break so the **second line carries the weight** — the noun, the payoff, the punch.

- `STRONGER FRAME\nFEWER PARTS` — second line is the payoff claim. Good.
- `STRONGER FRAME FEWER\nPARTS` — break orphans the noun from its modifier. Bad.

If the phrase has a comma, break at the comma:
- `MADE FOR PROS,\nUSED BY ALL` — break at the comma. Good.

If the phrase has no natural pivot and is only 3 words, break before the last word:
- `BUILT TO\nLAST`
- `ENGINEERED TO\nOUTLAST`

## Forbidden shapes

- Questions: ~~`READY TO LEVEL UP?`~~
- Exclamations: ~~`PUSH IT HARDER!`~~
- Hype-bro slang: ~~`THIS GEAR SLAPS`~~
- Two complete sentences: ~~`IT'S BUILT TO LAST. YOU'RE GOING TO LOVE IT.`~~
