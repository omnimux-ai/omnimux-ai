# Regrade prompt fragments

Copy these verbatim into the `style_direction` field. There are two variants — one for lifestyle slides (any slide with a human subject) and one for product-spec slides (clean hero shot, no human).

## Lifestyle slides (default)

> Premium athletic editorial. Dark/dramatic treatment with cinematic high contrast — crush shadows, hold motivated rim highlights on subjects, desaturate mid-tones toward charcoal/teal, heavy edge vignette so the headline reads cleanly in the lower third. Sharp gritty texture; no soft beauty diffusion. No HDR sheen. Subject sits in the upper two-thirds; lower third crushed dark enough for white headline text to read without an outline.

## Product-spec slides (clean hero, no human)

> Studio spotlight on the product. Single hard directional key from upper-camera-left, deep falloff into near-black. Background crushed to charcoal. Specular highlights held on metal / material edges to read material density. Subtle floor reflection acceptable. Same edge vignette feel as the lifestyle slides so the carousel reads as a cohesive set. No soft fill, no HDR sheen, no glossy advertising look.

## Banner block (paste into every slide's TEXT OVERLAYS section)

> Bottom banner: solid near-black panel, full frame width, height ≈ 12% of frame height, flush at very bottom.
> LEFT: brand logo preserved verbatim from LOGO REFERENCE asset, vertically centered, left padding ≈ 4% of frame width, logo height ≈ 70% of banner height. Do not redraw the wordmark.
> RIGHT: solid colored sharp-rounded pill (corner radius ≈ 50% of pill height — rounded but not bubbly / not perfect-capsule). Vertically centered, right padding ≈ 4% of frame width. CTA copy in bold white sans-serif, vertically centered inside the pill.

## Headline block (paste into every slide's TEXT OVERLAYS section)

> Headline: large bold uppercase white condensed sans-serif (Anton-style), lower third of image area (above the banner), centered, two lines with explicit `\n` break at a natural phrase boundary. Subtle soft drop shadow only — no outline, no stroke. Background underneath must be crushed dark enough for the text to read without a stroke.
