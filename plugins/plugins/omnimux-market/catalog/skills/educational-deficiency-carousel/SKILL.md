# Non-English Deficiency Carousel (3-Slide Educational Format)

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Ideal for:**
- Wellness / superfood brands targeting health-conscious localized-language audiences
- Instagram feed carousels (square 1:1) where education builds brand authority
- Topics structured as: *what is the problem → what does it do to the body → what fixes it*
- Brands that own a single hero raw ingredient and want to rotate it across multiple benefit claims

**Not suited for:**
- Promotional or sale-price content
- Video or Reel formats
- Audiences that require English copy (the on-image language is a localized non-English language; internal prompts stay English)
- More than one product per carousel — this pattern is a single-ingredient reveal, not a range

---

## 2. Hook & Opening — Slide 1 (The Definition)

**Structural move:** *The Named Problem Opener.* Slide 1 presents the topic as a serious, clinical concept — not a fear tactic, just a dignified statement of fact. The dominant typographic element is the topic word in very large bold serif, commanding the whole canvas. The sub-deck is a calm definition.

**Generic example (invented, for a neutral hypothetical product):**
> An opening line such as `"What is [Topic]-deficiency?"` rendered in the localized language — a large elegant serif lead-in, followed by an oversized bold serif `"[Topic]deficiency?"` compound word dominating the lower two-thirds, with a muted subtitle explaining the concept in one sentence.

**Swap rule:** Replace `[Topic]` (any nutrient or topic name) and its associated grammatical/compound forms in the target language. Keep the `"What is [Topic]-deficiency?"` construction exactly. Keep all colors, textures, and hierarchy.

**Background:** Deep dark-tone background (a near-black green/blue), linen/grain texture, ghost botanical fronds at ~10% opacity in the lower third.

**Label:** A short series label — tiny all-caps, accent color, wide letter-spacing, top-center. Acts as a series label, not a headline. Never change this label once chosen for the series.

---

## 3. Narrative Arc

| Beat | Slide | Target content | Tone |
|------|-------|----------------|------|
| 1 — Define | Slide 1 | Name the problem. Clinical, dignified. | Dark, deep, authoritative |
| 2 — Agitate | Slide 2 | 3 bullet symptoms / body impact. No hysteria — factual, empathetic. | Light, airy, grounded |
| 3 — Resolve | Slide 3 | Name the hero ingredient. Position as nature's richest natural source. | Warm accent tone, botanical reverence |

The arc is always **Define → Impact → Ingredient**. The three slides are dispatched in parallel (they are visually independent) but must tell this story in sequence when swiped.

---

## 4. Visual Style Spec

### Global rules (all 3 slides)
- Square canvas: **1080 × 1080 px**
- Aesthetic: premium editorial magazine × botanical archive × documentary photography
- No product packaging. No pills, jars, or supplement bottles. Ever.
- No artificial gloss, no lifestyle stock photography, no models
- Brand logo as **watermark only** — never as a focal element
- Ghost botanical texture present on every slide at ≤10% opacity

### Slide 1 — The Definition
| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Background | Deep dark-tone background (near-black green/blue) |
| Texture | Linen/grain overlay |
| Botanical ghost | Lower-third fronds, ~10% opacity |
| Series label | Short series label — accent color, tiny all-caps, wide tracking, top-center |
| Headline | `"What is"` lead-in — large elegant serif, light/cream tone |
| Topic word | `"[Topic]deficiency?"` — very large bold serif, light/cream tone, dominant |
| Rule | Accent hairline divider below headline block |
| Body | Muted one-line subtitle defining the condition |
| Watermark | Brand wordmark, bottom-left, white ~30% opacity |

### Slide 2 — The Impact
| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Background | Light sand/cream tone |
| Texture | Paper grain overlay |
| Botanical ghost | Left-edge, desaturated, ~10% opacity |
| Section label | Section label — tiny all-caps, dark tone, top-left |
| Heading | 2-line bold serif stating the problem's impact on the body, dark tone |
| Rule | Accent hairline |
| Bullet symptoms | 3 × arrow `→` bullet points, semibold sans, dark tone |
| Source note | Italic, muted accent, small — a generic credible source citation in the target language |
| Watermark | Brand icon + wordmark, bottom-left, dark tone ~25% opacity |

### Slide 3 — The Biological Solution
| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Background | Warm accent tone (muted earthy mid-tone) |
| Texture | Linen overlay |
| Hero image | Ultra-realistic macro photo of the raw hero ingredient — left/bottom-left 40% of canvas, on wet natural rock, glistening, moody cinematic lighting |
| Section label | Section label — tiny all-caps, white, top |
| Ingredient name | Italic bold serif, dominant, white — the common name in the target language |
| Sub-heading | `"The richest natural source of [Nutrient] on Earth"` (in the target language) — semibold sans, white |
| Body | 1–2 lines of body copy describing the mechanism |
| Scientific name | Small-caps, white 50% opacity, bottom-right |
| Watermark | Brand icon + wordmark, bottom-left, white ~30% opacity |

### Typography system
| Role | Typeface |
|------|----------|
| Headings / serifs | Playfair Display or Editorial New |
| Body / sans | Montserrat or Inter |

### Color palette
Use a three-tone palette aligned to the three slides — one deep dark tone (Slide 1),
one light sand/cream (Slide 2), and one warm muted accent (Slide 3). Pick the
exact hex values to match the brand's own palette; the structural requirement is
the dark → light → warm-accent progression across the three slides, not any
specific hex.

## 5. Voice & Persona

- **Language:** All on-image text in the target localized **non-English language**. Internal strategy and prompts in English.
- **Tone:** Calm-authoritative. Educational, not promotional. No aggressive hooks. No urgency language. No superlatives like "The best!" or "Buy now!".
- **Persona archetype:** The knowledgeable naturalist — someone who respects both science and nature, cites sources quietly, and lets the ingredient speak for itself.
- **Phrasing style:** Short declarative sentences. Noun-heavy phrasing in the target language. Avoid exclamation marks entirely.
- **Series label** — always present on Slide 1; signals this is an ongoing educational series. Never translate or abbreviate it once set.

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

There is **no explicit CTA** on any slide. The educational format itself is the engagement mechanic — the carousel rewards curiosity with knowledge. The brand name and logo watermark do the brand attribution passively.

If a CTA is added in future iterations, it must be a soft sign-off on Slide 3 only, in small-caps, white, below the body copy — e.g. a localized "Learn more → Link in bio". It must never interrupt the botanical calm of the slide.

---

## 7. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

Treat these as non-negotiable:

1. **Exactly 3 slides per topic. No more, no less.** The definition → impact → solution arc is complete at 3. Do not add a fourth "product" slide.
2. **No product packaging on any slide.** The Slide 3 hero is always the raw, unprocessed ingredient — never a jar, pouch, or supplement bottle.
3. **All on-image text is in the target non-English language.** Even if the prompt arrives in English, all visible copy on the rendered image must be in the target language.
4. **Series label appears on Slide 1 every time.** It is the series anchor — do not omit, translate, or resize it.
5. **Ghost botanical texture at ≤10% opacity.** It is a texture, not a graphic element. If it reads as a visible illustration, it is too strong.
6. **No sales language.** Words equivalent to "buy", "order", "offer", "discount", "sale" are banned from all slides in any language.
7. **Brand logo as watermark only.** Opacity ≤30% on dark slides, ≤25% on light slides. Never centered, never large, never the primary visual.
8. **QA gate before delivery.** All 3 slides pass `ad_image_qa` with a passing score before being considered final. Do not deliver slides that fail QA.
9. **Slides are produced in parallel.** All three image generation calls are dispatched simultaneously; do not chain them sequentially.
10. **Ingredient mapping is fixed per series.** Maintain a canonical topic → ingredient mapping for the series so the same topic always resolves to the same hero ingredient. Define this mapping once per brand and reuse it.

---

## 8. New Topic Checklist

When running this pattern for a new topic, confirm:

- [ ] Topic name and its compound form in the target language (`[Topic]deficiency`)
- [ ] 3 symptom bullets for Slide 2 in the target language
- [ ] Correct hero ingredient for Slide 3 (per the series mapping)
- [ ] Scientific name for the ingredient (Slide 3 bottom-right)
- [ ] `"The richest natural source of [Nutrient] on Earth"` sub-heading is grammatically correct in the target language for this nutrient
- [ ] Brand logo asset available as input image for watermark compositing
- [ ] QA run on all 3 outputs before delivery
