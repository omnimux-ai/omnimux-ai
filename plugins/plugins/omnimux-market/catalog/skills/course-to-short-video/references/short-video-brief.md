# Short Video Cut — Brief & Refinement Patterns

Template and parameters for cutting long-form educational videos into high-converting social shorts.

## Brief Structure
- **Target Platform**: TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts (9:16 vertical)
- **Duration**: 20-45 seconds
- **Subtitles**: High-contrast animated word-by-word captions
- **Pacing**: Fast-paced, complete thought arcs
- **End Card**: Course name + instructor call-to-action
