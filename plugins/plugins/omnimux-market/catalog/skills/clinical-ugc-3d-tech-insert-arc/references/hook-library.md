# Hook Library: Pain-Confession POV Questions

A bank of pill-style hook questions for the clinical-ugc-3d-tech-insert-arc pattern. Swap the pain site and environment to match your product. All examples below are generic templates — adapt them to your actual pain point.

## Wrist / Hand Strain
- "Why Do My Hands Go Numb By Noon?"
- "Why Do My Wrists Ache After Every Shift?"
- "Why Can't I Grip Anything By 3 PM?"

## Neck / Posture
- "Why Does My Neck Hurt Every Single Morning?"
- "Why Can't I Sit Upright for More Than an Hour?"
- "Why Does My Posture Feel 20 Years Older Than I Am?"

## Knee / Joint
- "Why Do My Knees Feel Like Bricks After Stairs?"
- "Why Does Every Step Hurt by Midday?"

## Formula
> **"Why Does [body part] [symptom] [time trigger]?"**

Keep the question under 8 words. Use "My" not "Your" — the confession is from the subject, not addressed to the viewer.
