# Local Service Static Batch Blitz

A single-session workflow that turns 3–6 real product photos + a brand brief into 15 distinct, Meta-ready 9:16 static ads — each with its own copy angle and layout archetype, dispatched in parallel.

---

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Best fit:**
- Local service businesses (equipment rental, landscaping, HVAC, plumbing, moving, roofing, cleaning, etc.)
- Brands with physical equipment, job-site photos, or before/after shots
- Meta Feed / Reels cover / Stories placements (9:16 vertical)
- Goal: saturate a creative library quickly — 15 ads, zero repetition

**Poor fit:**
- Pure digital / SaaS products with no tangible visual asset
- Clients who want video (use a video-batch skill instead)
- Fewer than 3 usable product photos (quality suffers below that threshold)

---

## 2. Hook & Opening

**Structural move: Slogan-hero lock.**  
The brand's own slogan becomes an ad-opening statement — typed in all-caps oversized type, usually 2-line split — treated as if it were a cultural headline, not a tagline.

**Generic worked example (invented for a neutral hypothetical brand):**
> Headline: BOOK IT.  
> Sub-line: WE HANDLE IT.  
> Layout: Full-bleed product photo, dark gradient from bottom, white Anton headline centered low.

**Swap the subject, keep the move:** Replace the slogan with the new brand's core action promise. If they have no slogan, construct one in subject–verb–object form. The two-line split and the oversized all-caps lock are non-negotiable.

**Other hook archetypes in the rotation (assign one per ad):**

| # | Hook archetype | Example headline |
|---|---------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Slogan hero | BOOK IT. WE HANDLE IT. |
| 2 | Convenience stat | Drop-off in 24 hrs. Pick-up when you're done. |
| 3 | Speed proof | Same-day delivery available. |
| 4 | Size selection | Small · Medium · Large — Pick Your Size. |
| 5 | Pricing transparency | Honest pricing. No hidden fees. |
| 6 | Family-owned authority | Family-Owned. Locally Operated. |
| 7 | Local authority | Serving [City] Since [Year]. |
| 8 | Urgency / scarcity | Limited availability. Book today. |
| 9 | Homeowner angle | Home project? We've got you. |
| 10 | Contractor angle | Built for job sites. Ready when you are. |
| 11 | Use-case proof A | Big job? Small job? One call. |
| 12 | Use-case proof B | Cleanups made easy. |
| 13 | Trust signals | Licensed · Insured · 5-Star Rated |
| 14 | Same-day hero | Need it today? We deliver. |
| 15 | Brand pillars | Reliable · Affordable · Local |

## 3. Narrative Arc

Static ads have no time axis, but each executes a **visual narrative in three zones** (top → middle → bottom):

| Zone | Role | Content |
|------|------|---------|
| **Top zone** | Attention / credibility hook | Brand name, location badge, or bold stat |
| **Middle zone** | Product proof / emotion | Hero product photo (always a real job-site shot) |
| **Bottom zone** | CTA + contact | CTA button style text + phone/website |

**Beat-level intent per ad:**
1. **Stop** — The headline or layout pattern must interrupt the scroll in ≤ 0.3 sec visual parse.
2. **Confirm** — Product photo confirms this is real, local, tangible.
3. **Convert** — CTA is unambiguous. One action only.

No ad should attempt more than one message. If it has a size-selection hook, the only CTA is "Check Availability Online." It does not also push the family-owned story.

## 4. Visual Style Spec

**Overall register:** Clean, professional, blue-collar/industrial. Not grungy. Not luxury. Think a well-designed trade contractor's truck wrap — bold, legible, trustworthy.

**Color palette anchors:**
- Primary: Dominant brand color
- Accent: White for headline type; yellow/amber for caution-stripe accents only
- Overlay mode: Dark gradient (not flat black box) when text sits over photo

**Camera grammar:**
- All photos are real job-site shots — never renderings or stock
- Use full-bleed whenever the photo has strong composition
- Split-panel when photo has a busy background that competes with copy

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Anton or Archivo Black — all caps, large, tracked normally
- Sublines / body: DM Sans or Open Sans — sentence case, moderate weight
- No script fonts. No decorative fonts. Legibility is the rule.
- On-screen text style: Bold overlay captions (not lower-thirds; not kinetic animation — this is static)

**Shot length / layout distribution across the 15-ad batch:**

| Layout archetype | # of ads | When to use |
|-----------------|-----------|-------------|
| Full-bleed + bottom gradient overlay | 3 | Strong hero photo, copy lives at bottom |
| Split panel (photo top / solid color bottom) | 2 | Busy photo backgrounds |
| Diagonal text band across full-bleed | 1 | Speed / urgency angle |
| Typography-led (giant numbers/words) + photo anchor | 1 | Size selection or stat angle |
| Clean two-zone (white panel top / photo bottom) | 1 | Pricing transparency or family-owned |
| Editorial full-bleed + warm gradient | 1 | Homeowner / friendly angle |
| Full-bleed + location badge list | 1 | Local authority angle |
| Solid color top zone + photo bottom (urgency) | 1 | Urgency / scarcity angle |
| Near-opaque dark overlay (contractor/industrial) | 1 | Contractor / job-site angle |
| White panel + photo (use-case proof) | 1 | Use-case proof angles |
| Vertical split (color panel left / photo right) | 1 | Trust signals / brand pillars |
| Three-zone (text / photo / trust badges) | 1 | Trust badge row |
| Full-bleed + caution stripe top | 1 | High-energy or same-day angle |

**Product photo rotation rule:** Distribute the available photos (3–6) evenly across 15 ads. No photo should appear more than 4 times. Each layout archetype should use a different photo when possible.

## 5. Voice & Persona

**Tone:** Calm-authoritative. Not salesy. Not hype. The brand speaks like a reliable local expert who has done this a thousand times and doesn't need to shout about it.

**Persona archetype:** Competent local operator — peer-to-peer, not corporate. Uses "you" and "we" freely.

**Pacing:** Copy is short. Headlines max 6 words. Sublines max 12 words. CTAs max 5 words.

**Sample phrasings that match this register:**
- "Drop-off today. Pick-up when you're done."
- "Book it. We handle it. That's it."
- "No hidden fees. No hassle. Just results."
- "Serving [City] homeowners and contractors for [X] years."
- "Book online in 60 seconds."

**Anti-patterns to avoid:**
- Exclamation points in every line (one per ad, max)
- "AMAZING" / "BEST EVER" superlatives without a proof signal
- Corporate jargon ("synergistic solutions", "leverage your project")

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**Pattern:** Single spoken/typed directive + friction-reducer + contact anchor.

**Four approved CTA forms (rotate across batch):**

| CTA form | Example | When to use |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Book Now | "Book Now → [website]" | Urgency / conversion ads |
| Reserve Online Today | "Reserve Online Today" | Convenience / planning ads |
| Get Instant Pricing | "Get Instant Pricing · [phone]" | Pricing transparency ads |
| Check Availability Online | "Check Availability Online" | Size selection / same-day ads |

**Hard rule:** Phone number and/or website appears on every ad, either in the CTA zone or as a lower badge. It is never the only element — always paired with a CTA verb.

**Placement:** Bottom 15–20% of frame. High contrast against background (white on dark, dark on white panel). Never centered in the visual field — always anchored to bottom.

---

## 7. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

These are the creative constraints locked in by this workflow. Treat every item as a non-negotiable:

1. **15 ads, all distinct.** Each ad gets exactly one copy angle and one layout archetype from the rotation tables. No two ads share the same angle + archetype combination.

2. **Parallel dispatch.** All 15 director calls go out at the same time. Do not serialize. This is a batch workflow — latency must be minimized.

3. **Real photos only.** Every ad uses one of the uploaded product photos as its primary visual. Do not generate synthetic product imagery or use stock. The photos are the brand's proof of legitimacy.

4. **9:16 aspect ratio, no exceptions.** Meta Feed + Stories vertical format. If a client mentions horizontal or square, confirm before deviating.

5. **No ad repeats a CTA.** Distribute the four CTA forms across the batch. Every ad has a CTA; no ad has two.

6. **Brand name on every ad.** Either as headline, badge, or watermark. It never disappears.

7. **Copy stays short.** Headline ≤ 6 words. Subline ≤ 12 words. Body text ≤ 20 words. If you're writing more, consolidate.

8. **Caution-stripe is accent only.** Yellow/amber caution stripe appears in at most 2 of the 15 ads. It is a spice, not a base coat.

9. **Product is always swappable.** The brand name, slogan, phone, website, service area, and product photos are all inputs — the mold accepts any local service brand. Do not hardcode any one brand's specifics into the pattern.

10. **Brief first, then dispatch.** Always write the 15-ad plan to a file (`/tmp/outputs/image-ad-brief.md` or equivalent) before dispatching any director calls. This prevents drift and gives the user a review checkpoint.
