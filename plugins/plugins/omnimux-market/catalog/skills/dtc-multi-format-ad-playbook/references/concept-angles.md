# In-Game DTC Concept Angle Menu

Eight tested angles for in-game DTC video ads. Mix at least 3 across a launch.

---

## 1. Print Parade / Variety Showcase

**When to use:** catalog-breadth brand (many prints, colorways, sizes). Anchors identity-recognition. Best for apparel, totes, accessories, home textiles.

**Length:** ~35s, 6–7 beats, 5s each + CTA card.

**Persona:** one on-camera persona, same outfit-style, same setting. Mid-shot, looks at camera, smiles, holds the product. Rotate persona ethnicity/age vs. other ads in the set.

**Hook archetype:** identity-recognition. Template — *"Tell me you're a [audience identity] without telling me you're a [audience identity]."* Persona delivers it once at the top; the rest of the ad is the *visual proof* (one new print per beat).

**Music feel:** warm Americana acoustic, mid-tempo, smile-in-music.

**Beat shape:**
1. Hook line + product #1 (5s)
2–6. Same persona, same setting, products #2–#6 (5s each, near-cut transitions)
7. CTA card (5–7s)

**Watch out for:** if you only have 2–3 prints, this angle collapses — pick Single-Product Feature instead.

---

## 2. Single-Product Feature Ad

**When to use:** one hero SKU you want to *sell* (not just *show*). Pushes detail, material, capacity, USP.

**Length:** ~30s, 5 beats, ~6s each.

**Persona:** late-20s/early-30s, peer voice, UGC-pulse handheld. Slight rasp / authentic. Or persona-less product-only with VO narrator.

**Hook archetype:** product-as-character close-up — slow push-in on material/texture *before* the reveal. Caption asks a question ("What is this material?" / "Why does this feel like this?").

**Music feel:** upbeat tropical pop / modern UGC bed.

**Beat shape:**
1. Hook (product question or texture push-in) — 6s
2. Material / texture macro + tactile VO — 6s
3. Capacity / utility shot (in use) — 6s
4. Interior or unexpected-detail beat — 6s
5. CTA card — 5–7s

**Watch out for:** if the SKU has no tactile / detail story, pick POV Diary instead — that angle lets the lifestyle do the selling.

---

## 3. Gift Reveal / Emotional Unboxing

**When to use:** Q4, Mother's Day, Valentine's, birthdays, anniversary. Anything where the product *is a gift*.

**Length:** ~33s, 6 beats.

**Persona:** TWO personas — a **narrator** (giver / partner, voice-only via `voice_only=true`, warm, intimate, whispered) and a **recipient** (on-camera reaction; this is the money shot in beat 3).

**Hook archetype:** emotional setup — wrapped present, hands only, soft piano. No copy yet. Caption fades in: *"They have no idea what's inside."*

**Music feel:** emotional piano, slow build.

**Beat shape:**
1. Wrapped gift + narrator setup — 5s
2. Unwrap action, hands only — 5s
3. Recipient's emotional reaction — 6s (money shot, hold longer than you think)
4. Embroidery / material detail macro — 5s
5. Variety menu (2–3 alternate prints / colorways) — 6s
6. CTA card — 5–7s

**Watch out for:** if the recipient's reaction reads as performative, the ad dies. Cast (or describe) for *understated*, not *theatrical*.

---

## 4. POV Diary / One Day In The Life

**When to use:** lifestyle-aspirational positioning. Product is *with you* through a day; never the subject, always the companion.

**Length:** ~30s, 6 beats, ~5s each.

**Persona:** first-person POV (hands, feet, shoulder, never face — or only a glimpse). Narrator is the persona's own inner voice, calm, present-tense, journaling.

**Hook archetype:** POV cold-open. No narrator yet, just sound of morning. Caption fades in by ~3s: *"[Day]. [Time]. [Event identifier]."* (e.g. "Monday. 6am. Trail day.")

**Music feel:** warm indie-folk, finger-picked guitar.

**Beat shape:**
1. Morning (window, coffee, bag on chair) — 5s
2. Leaving home (door, keys, bag on shoulder) — 5s
3. Midday errand (market, café, school run) — 5s
4. Core use moment (beach, picnic, gym) — 5s
5. Evening (sunset, dinner, drive home) — 5s
6. CTA card — 5–7s

**Watch out for:** showing the persona's face too early breaks the POV illusion. Keep face out until the core-use beat, if at all.

## 5. Identity Hook (extension of Print Parade for non-catalog brands)

**When to use:** brand has ONE clear audience identity (e.g. "ocean people", "remote workers", "first-time parents") but doesn't have catalog breadth.

**Length:** ~30s.

**Hook archetype:** same identity-recognition line as Print Parade, but the proof is *moments* (3–4 lifestyle vignettes), not *products*.

**Beat shape:**
1. Hook line + persona reveal — 5s
2–4. Three lifestyle vignettes that *prove* the identity — 6–7s each
5. CTA card — 5–7s

---

## 6. Founder UGC

**When to use:** brand has a real, on-camera-comfortable founder with a story. Especially strong for women-founded apparel, small-batch beauty, niche home goods.

**Length:** ~35–45s (longer is fine here — founders earn extra dwell).

**Hook archetype:** founder direct-to-camera, handheld, semi-cluttered authentic background. Template — *"I started this because [specific personal moment]…"*. Never start with the product name.

**Beat shape:**
1. Founder intro / origin moment — 8s
2. Problem the product solves (founder's POV) — 8s
3. Hands-on product reveal — 8s
4. Soft social proof (one customer quote on-screen, or one stat) — 6s
5. CTA card — 5–7s

**Music feel:** minimal / under-spoken. Let the voice carry.

---

## 7. Pattern Montage

**When to use:** the product has visual *graphic* appeal (prints, colorways, embroidery) and you want a kinetic edit. Good as the "energy variant" in a launch set.

**Length:** ~25–30s.

**Hook archetype:** zero VO at top. Rapid-cut close-ups (12–15 shots, each 1.5–2s) of patterns / textures / colorways, on a beat-matched track. VO and CTA enter only in the last 8s.

**Music feel:** beat-driven, drop in the final third.

**Beat shape:**
1. Pattern montage (no VO, on-beat cuts) — 18–20s
2. Brand reveal + tagline VO — 5s
3. CTA card — 5–7s

**Watch out for:** this is the *only* angle that legitimately uses <3s cuts. Don't import the pace into other angles.

---

## 8. Before / After Split or Reveal

**When to use:** product has a clear before/after story (organization, beauty, fit, mood). Apparel can use it (closet-mess → outfit-with-bag → out-the-door).

**Length:** ~28–32s.

**Hook archetype:** the *before* state, exaggerated lightly. Caption: *"This was every morning."*

**Beat shape:**
1. Before state — 6s
2. Product enters — 5s
3. After state, repeated (a few days / a week passing) — 10s
4. Insight VO line — 4s
5. CTA card — 5–7s
