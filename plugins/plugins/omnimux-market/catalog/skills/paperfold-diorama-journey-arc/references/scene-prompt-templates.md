# Scene Prompt Templates — Paperfold Diorama Journey Arc

Copy-paste these as starting points for each beat's image generation. Replace `[BRACKETED]` values with product/brand specifics. Keep all paper-craft language verbatim.

---

## Beat 1 — Problem (Stress / Struggle)

```
Everything in the scene is made of folded paper — the couple, their clothing, their hair,
the furniture, the [bills / documents / objects representing the problem], even the warm
overhead light. Geometric origami-style facets throughout. A [man and woman / person]
sit at a [kitchen table / desk], shoulders slightly hunched, expressions showing quiet
worry. Paper [bills / documents] are spread across the table. The entire image looks like
an intricate paper diorama or paper sculpture photographed in a studio. Warm cream and
muted teal color palette. Soft, intimate lighting. Aspect ratio 9:16, cinematic.
```

**Camera motion:** Slow push-in
**VO tone:** Confessional, first-person plural

---

## Beat 2 — Aspiration Blocked (Exterior / Wider)

```
Everything in the scene is made of folded paper — the couple, their clothing, their hair,
the [house / building / environment], the [FOR SALE sign / obstacle object], the trees,
the sky, even the clouds. Geometric origami-style facets throughout. A [man and woman /
person] stand outside a paper [house / location], looking up at it with a mix of longing
and uncertainty. The environment feels slightly large around them, emphasizing the obstacle.
The entire image looks like an intricate paper diorama or paper sculpture photographed in
a studio. Warm cream, soft blue, and muted gold color palette. Bright but slightly cool
exterior light. Aspect ratio 9:16, cinematic.
```

**Camera motion:** Pull-back or locked-off
**VO tone:** Frustrated but not defeated; one concrete obstacle named

---

## Beat 3 — Discovery / Solution (Over-the-Shoulder Screen POV)

```
Everything in the scene is made of folded paper — the couple, their clothing, their hair,
the [laptop / tablet / phone], the desk or surface, the room environment. Geometric
origami-style facets throughout. Shot from over the shoulder of the [man / woman], looking
at a paper laptop screen that displays a paper value comparison: the old value [X] crossed
out in red paper, and the new better value [Y] highlighted in teal paper. The other person
leans in, expression shifting from worry to hope. The entire image looks like an intricate
paper diorama or paper sculpture photographed in a studio. Warm cream and muted teal color
palette. Soft screen-glow light on their faces. Aspect ratio 9:16, cinematic.
```

**Camera motion:** Slow push-in toward screen
**VO tone:** Curiosity shifting to relief; product named once, naturally
**Critical:** Over-the-shoulder angle only. No front-facing couple shot. Value comparison must be visible on the paper screen.

---

## Beat 4 — Resolution / Joy (Celebration / Reveal)

```
Everything in the scene is made of folded paper — the couple, their clothing, their hair,
the [SOLD sign / open door / celebration object], the [house / environment], confetti or
light rays if applicable. Geometric origami-style facets throughout. A [man and woman /
person] [embrace / cheer / hold up keys] in front of a paper [SOLD sign / open door /
milestone marker], expressions bright and joyful. The composition is open and wide,
figures upright and energized. The entire image looks like an intricate paper diorama or
paper sculpture photographed in a studio. Warm cream, muted gold, and soft teal color
palette. Bright, warm, celebratory lighting. Aspect ratio 9:16, cinematic.
```

**Camera motion:** Pull-back to reveal
**VO tone:** Warm, grateful; short declarative sentences

---

## Beat 5 — CTA / Deal Close (Handshake / Brand Moment)

```
Everything in the scene is made of folded paper — the [couple / person], their clothing,
their hair, the [desk / table], the [documents / handshake / brand object], the
environment. Geometric origami-style facets throughout. A [handshake / document signing /
couple smiling at camera] moment, calm and resolved. The composition is clean and
slightly formal — this is the close. The entire image looks like an intricate paper
diorama or paper sculpture photographed in a studio. Warm cream and muted teal color
palette. Soft, even studio lighting. Aspect ratio 9:16, cinematic.
```

**Camera motion:** Locked-off or very slow push-in
**VO tone:** Calm, direct; brand URL spoken
**On-screen:** Teal pill CTA card with brand URL overlaid in post

## Character consistency checklist

Before generating any image, lock these attributes and repeat them in every prompt:

- Hair color: `[e.g., warm brown / dark / blonde]`
- Clothing color (person A): `[e.g., soft blue shirt]`
- Clothing color (person B): `[e.g., cream blouse]`
- Build: `[e.g., average build, mid-30s]`

Add a line to every prompt: *"Same characters as established: [hair color] hair, [clothing description], [build]."*

## Music brief template

```
Warm piano and soft strings, instrumental only, no vocals. Approximately [30–35] seconds.
Emotional arc: starts tender and quiet (Beats 1–2), builds gently through discovery
(Beat 3), reaches warmest/most uplifting peak at resolution (Beat 4), settles to calm
close (Beat 5). Hopeful, not triumphant. Cinematic, not pop.
```

**Music volume in assembly:** 0.12 (background texture only)
