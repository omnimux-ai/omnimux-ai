# Narrative Arc Library (pick one per product)

Each arc is a high-end stylized-3D short-form shape. Pick the one whose **climax** best dramatizes the product's core benefit, then make the benefit literal at that climax. Default shot count 4 for ≤30s; expand to 5–6 for 45s.

## 1. Curiosity-hook → escalation → surprise transformation
- Beat A (hook): an intriguing, slightly mysterious image that withholds context.
- Beat B (reveal/escalation): show the absurd object/device; stakes rise.
- Beat C (tension): hesitation or conflict before the leap.
- Beat D (payoff): the transformation — **benefit made literal**.
- Best for: power-ups, dramatic upgrades, "level up" benefits.

## 2. Underdog wants something → obstacle → clever win
- Beat A: lovable underdog has a clear desire.
- Beat B: an obstacle blocks them (mundane but cruel).
- Beat C: the product enables a clever, surprising solution.
- Beat D: the win, with a charm beat.
- Best for: products that help you overcome a daily struggle.

## 3. Setup → misdirection → comedic twist / subversion
- Beat A: establish an expectation.
- Beat B: lean into the expected path (misdirection).
- Beat C: subvert it — the product makes the real outcome better/funnier than expected.
- Best for: "not what you think" positioning, category disruptors.

## 4. Emotional micro-arc (loneliness → connection)
- Beat A: a small, lonely or incomplete moment.
- Beat B: the product appears as a bridge.
- Beat C: connection/warmth lands; gentle smile, not a gag.
- Best for: social, sharing, comfort, food-to-share products.

## 5. Rivalry → one-upmanship → mutual payoff
- Beat A: two characters compete.
- Beat B: escalating one-upmanship.
- Beat C: the product makes **both** win — shared payoff.
- Best for: duo/sharing products, "everyone wins" messaging.

## 6. Day-in-the-life mundane loop → absurd disruption → new normal
- Beat A: establish a dull repeating routine.
- Beat B: the product disrupts it absurdly.
- Beat C: a delightful new normal settles in.
- Best for: products that change a daily habit or routine.

## Mapping rule
Climax = benefit made visible. Write the climax first, then back-fill the earlier beats so the payoff is earned. Keep pacing slow-burn → escalation → payoff regardless of which arc you choose.
