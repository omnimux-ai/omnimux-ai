# Landing Page DR — COD / Cash-on-Delivery E-commerce

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Best fit:**
- Health, wellness, pharmaceutical-adjacent, beauty, or nutraceutical products sold via cash-on-delivery (COD / contraentrega) in LATAM markets
- Single-product landing pages where the full funnel must fit in a vertical image set (no web dev available)
- Brands that need a complete trust-building sequence: problem → proof → social validation → offer → logistics trust

**Not a fit:**
- Fashion / apparel (different visual grammar)
- SaaS or digital products (no logistics trust section needed)
- Multi-SKU catalogues (the funnel is designed for one hero product)

## 2. Hook & Opening — Image 1: HERO / Above the Fold

**Structural move:** *Transformation ghost + credentialed product debut.*  
The hero image shows the product prominently alongside a ghosted before/after silhouette — one figure in pain (hunched, red glow at pain point), one standing upright in relief. The visual communicates the transformation without a single word of body copy. The headline sits above, short and punchy.

**Invented generic example (neutral hypothetical product — a topical relief gel):**
> Headline: "ADIÓS A LA MOLESTIA"  
> Sub: "Fórmula avanzada con ingrediente activo + complejo vitamínico"  
> 4 benefit icons: Acción Rápida · Sin Irritación · Doble Acción · Alivio Prolongado  
> Trust seals: Registro Sanitario · Origen Certificado · Envío Gratis · Pago Contraentrega

**Swap rule:** Replace the pain condition and product name. Keep the transformation ghost, the 4-icon row, and all four trust seals. The origin badge stays whenever the product's country of manufacture is verified — never fabricate a local origin if the product is foreign-made; state the true country of manufacture.

## 4. Visual Style Spec

### Color palette (default for health/pharma DR)
| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Primary background (dark sections) | `#0A1628` | Deep navy — images 1, 4, 5, 6, 8, 11 |
| Accent / highlight | `#F4A015` | Warm amber — CTAs, highlighted column, badges |
| Light section background | `#FEFAF3` | Warm cream — images 2, 3, 7, 9, 10 |
| Danger / pain indicator | `#C0392B` | Red — pain halos, negative column in comparison |

*For non-health products: adapt palette while keeping the dark/light alternation rhythm.*

### Camera grammar
- **Lifestyle / person images** (doctor, testimonial, manos): Photorealistic, warm directional lighting, shallow depth of field, subject at 60–70% frame height, product held LARGE and prominent in frame — never small or tucked to the side.
- **Graphic/design images** (hero, comparison, FAQ, offers, ingredients): Flat-lit studio aesthetic; UI-style layout with clear grid; product isolated on gradient or dark field.
- **Section alternation:** Dark navy → Light cream → Dark navy → Light cream... (strictly alternated to create visual breathing rhythm)

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold condensed, 5–8 words max, all-caps or title-case, high contrast on background
- **Supporting copy:** 1–2 short lines, sentence case, 60–70% opacity if on dark bg
- **Icon labels:** 2–4 words, tight tracking
- **No wall-of-text:** If a first-pass image has large paragraphs, it violates this spec — trim to headline + subtitle + short labels only.

### Shot length / information density
- Hero: medium information (headline + 4 icons + 4 seals)
- Ingredients / FAQs: highest information (4–5 cards with descriptions)
- Doctor / Testimonio: lowest information density (photo-dominant, minimal text overlay)

## 5. Voice & Persona

**Tone:** Calm-authoritative with warmth. Never hype. Never exclamation-mark-heavy. The product speaks through evidence (doctor, ingredients, numbers) not enthusiasm.

**Persona archetype:** *Trusted health advisor* — the brand knows the customer's pain intimately and has a solution that works. The doctor image is the peak authority moment; the testimonial image is the peer empathy moment.

**Language:** All text in Spanish. Neutral LATAM Spanish register (not regional slang). Formal enough for a pharmaceutical context, warm enough for COD/social commerce.

**Sample phrasing patterns (invented generic placeholders):**
- Headlines: "ADIÓS A [PROBLEMA]" / "CUANDO LA MOLESTIA NO TE DEJA VIVIR" / "LO QUE DICEN NUESTROS CLIENTES"
- Doctor quote: short and factual — "Recomiendo [product] porque actúa donde se necesita y cuida tu bienestar."
- Testimonial: first-person, specific, believable — "Llevaba mucho tiempo con la misma molestia. Desde las primeras semanas noté la diferencia."
- CTA button: "PEDIR AHORA — ENVÍO GRATIS" or "ORDENAR CON PAGO CONTRAENTREGA"

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**Placement:** Image 11 (Cierre) has the primary large CTA button. Image 9 (Ofertas) has a secondary CTA under each pricing card.

**Mechanic:** On-screen button (not spoken, not comment-bait). Button is amber on dark background, full-width, uppercase, includes shipping reinforcement in the label.

**Logistics trust strip** (always present in Image 11):
- Carrier logos: the region's major last-mile delivery carriers (use the actual carriers serving the product's market)
- Badges: Envío Gratis · Pago Contraentrega · Entrega 2–5 días
- Emotional close line before button (invented generic example): "Miles de personas ya viven mejor. Es tu turno."

## 7. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

These are absolute constraints — treat each as locked:

1. **NEVER use the horizontal box** — always use the vertical product box. If a horizontal box appears in any image, replace it with **2 oval soft gelatin capsules** (matching the product's actual capsule color/shape).
2. **Origin badge must be truthful** — state the true country of manufacture; never fabricate a local origin for a foreign-made product.
3. **Text balance:** Not too much (long paragraphs), not too little (only a headline). Each image must have: headline + subtitle/supporting line + short descriptive labels or list items.
4. **Product must appear in most images** — held in real human hands or prominently displayed. Never floating in isolation as a tiny element.
5. **Comparison table is an infographic** — 3-column visual with the center column (your product) elevated in amber, competitors flanking in gray. NOT a plain text table.
6. **Ingredient circles are REALISTIC MACRO PHOTOS** — actual crystalline/powder appearance of each ingredient. Not icons. Not cartoon circles.
7. **Doctor image: product held LARGE** — the box must be prominent, taking up significant hand/frame space. Not a small prop.
8. **ONE deep testimonial** — a single full-card testimonial with a long authentic quote, verified badge, and rating row below. Do not use two shallow side-by-side testimonials.
9. **Prueba Social (image 8): no horizontal box** — decorative elements are 2 oval capsules in a corner, not a product box.
10. **Offers pricing card layout:** the middle/2-pack tier is CENTER and LARGEST (amber highlight, "MÁS POPULAR" badge). The entry and complete-treatment tiers are smaller flanking cards. The middle tier is never an afterthought.
11. **FAQs: all 5 questions fully visible** — no collapsed dropdowns. Each answer is 2–3 lines visible in the image.

## 9. Product Facts Embedding

For each new product, identify and embed throughout relevant images:
- Units per package + treatment duration
- Time to first noticeable effect
- Active ingredients (with their physical appearance for Image 4)
- Certification / registration number
- Country of manufacture
- Accepted payment method + shipping carriers + coverage area
- Pricing tiers (3 options: entry / most-popular / complete-treatment)

The subject (product, brand, condition) is **always swappable**. The structure, visual grammar, voice, and hard rules above stay locked.
