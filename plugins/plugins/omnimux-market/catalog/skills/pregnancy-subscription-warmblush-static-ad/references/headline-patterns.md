# Headline Pattern Library

## Approved Pattern Structures

### Celebration Frame (Primary)
`[Celebration verb] + [Milestone person or moment]`

| Example | Audience | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------|
| "The Perfect Milestone Gift" | Gift-giver | Names social role + product positioning |
| "Made for This Moment" | Milestone person | Milestone emotion, no product specs |
| "Celebrate Them" | Gift-giver (partner/family) | Ultra-short, directive |
| "The Most-Loved [Category] Box" | Social proof seekers | Peer validation frame |

> These are illustrative molds, not approved copy — write fresh headlines per product.

### Gift-Giver Frame
`[Social role action] + [Recipient's milestone]`

Works for: partners, family members buying for someone reaching a milestone, friends

### Social Proof Frame
`"The Most-Loved [Category]"` or `"Loved by [Credible source]"`

Use when featuring award badges. Anchors credibility claim in the headline.

---

## Rejected Patterns (Hard No)

| Rejected headline | Why rejected |
|-------------------|--------------|
| "Customers Agree: We're the Best" | Self-congratulatory; brand-centric not recipient-centric |
| "Give the Gift They Actually Want" | Holiday/occasion coded, not evergreen |
| "Everything They Need Month by Month" | Functional checklist tone, off-brand |
| "Essentials Delivered to Their Door" | Delivery pitch, not emotional hook |

---

## Swap Rule

When adapting to a new product, swap the milestone noun:
- `"The Perfect [NEW MILESTONE] Gift"`
- `"Made for This [NEW MOMENT]"`
- `"Celebrate [NEW RECIPIENT]"`

Keep the verb, the emotional warmth, and the brevity. Never add features or delivery language.

## Typography Spec

| Element | Font style | Weight | Notes |
|---------|-----------|--------|-------|
| Primary headline | Clean rounded geometric sans-serif | Bold | Poppins, Nunito, DM Sans — match brand site |
| Secondary/subhead | Same family, lighter | Regular or Medium | |
| Offer badges | Bubbly/handwritten | Bold | ONLY inside sticker badges |
| URL/brand anchor | Clean sans-serif | Regular | Soft teal, small |

**Never use:** bubble letters, serif fonts on lifestyle hero ads, script/cursive for headlines.
