# UGC Inherited-Burden Testimonial Pattern

A 25–40-second vertical ad where a relatable everyday person describes a specific life-event burden, experiences the product solving it, and closes with a warm direct CTA. No actors, no studio polish — handheld phone aesthetic throughout.

---

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Best fit:**
- Products that remove friction from a stressful life event: cash home buyers, probate attorneys, debt relief, medical billing advocates, moving services, estate liquidators, tax resolution firms
- Audiences: homeowners 35–65, adults navigating family estate situations, financially stressed households
- Platforms: Meta (Reels/Feed), TikTok, YouTube Shorts

**Poor fit:**
- Luxury or aspirational products (cars, fashion, travel) — the burden-relief arc feels off-brand
- B2B SaaS or professional services to a sophisticated buyer
- Products that require demonstration (requires hands/product on screen, not a talking head)

---

## 2. Hook & Opening (0–2 s)

**Structural move: First-person specific-event confession**

The opener names a single concrete life event in the speaker's own voice — not a category, a specific moment. It should be specific enough to trigger recognition in the target viewer, yet universal enough to have happened to millions.

**Invented example (neutral, hypothetical product):**
> *"I just inherited a house I never expected…"*

**The move:** Subject + possessive relationship + object. Three words of setup that carry emotional weight without explaining anything. The viewer mentally finishes the sentence before the speaker does.

**Swap rule:** Replace the subject/relationship/object with the new product's trigger event. Keep the first-person possessive syntax and the trailing ellipsis pacing (spoken pause, not literal).

| New product | Hook swap |
|---|---|
| Debt relief | "I got hit with a $40,000 medical bill…" |
| Moving service | "My company transferred me across the country in three weeks…" |
| Tax resolution | "I got a letter from the tax office I didn't understand…" |
| Probate attorney | "A relative passed and left no will…" |

**Hard rule:** The hook must be a situation, not a product claim. Never open with the brand name or a benefit statement.

---

## 3. Narrative Arc

Five beats. Total target: 28–38 seconds. Adjust scene durations to fit copy at **2.5–3.0 words per second** — verify before dispatch.

| Beat | Target duration | Content | Visual | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Hook** | 5–8 s | First-person confession of the trigger event | Talking head, handheld, natural window light | Warm, slightly hesitant — like telling a friend |
| **2 · Pain** | 5–8 s | List 2–3 specific logistical/emotional burdens the situation created | Talking head, same setup | Honest, relatable frustration — not dramatic |
| **3 · B-roll cutaway** | 3–5 s | Silent shot of the object at the center of the problem (house exterior, document pile, moving boxes) | Handheld drift or slow pan, no voiceover, no music | Quiet — lets the viewer feel the weight |
| **4 · Solution** | 7–10 s | How the product removed the burden — specific, concrete, 2–3 proof points | Talking head | Relief, warmth, slightly faster pace |
| **5 · CTA** | 6–9 s | Direct spoken ask + URL + warm sign-off | Talking head + on-screen URL overlay | Friendly, low-pressure, spoken like advice to a friend |

**Arc name:** Problem → Agitation → Silent Object → Product Relief → Peer CTA

---

## 4. Visual Style Spec

**Aesthetic:** UGC / organic phone video. Must NOT look produced.

| Parameter | Spec |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 vertical |
| Camera grammar | Handheld — slight drift, micro-shake acceptable. No gimbal-smooth, no dolly, no drone |
| Lighting | Natural window light, one-sided. No ring light, no softbox fill, no bokeh |
| Framing | Headshot to mid-chest. Speaker slightly off-center. Imperfect is correct |
| Color | No LUTs, no color grade. Natural, slightly warm. No desaturation |
| Background | Real-life domestic setting (kitchen, living room, porch). No branded backdrops |
| B-roll | Single generated exterior/object image; handheld pan or slow drift treatment |
| On-screen text / captions | Word-by-word, outlined-text preset, Montserrat. Follows speech rhythm |
| Brand watermark | Small pill badge (brand name), lower-right corner, all talking-head scenes |
| CTA overlay | Contrasting pill ("GET YOUR [OFFER/QUOTE/CALL]", brand color) bottom-center + URL lower-center, fade in at ~2 s into CTA scene only |
| Music | **None.** Silence preserves UGC authenticity. Do not add background track |

---

## 5. Voice & Persona

**Persona archetype:** Relatable Everyday Peer — not an expert, not a celebrity. Someone who could be the viewer's neighbor or sibling.

| Parameter | Spec |
|---|---|
| Age range | Late 20s – early 40s |
| Aesthetic | Casual, put-together. Neutral-color crewneck or top, minimal jewelry (stud earrings, small pendant) |
| Setting | Home interior — kitchen, living room. Practical, lived-in |
| Voice tone | Warm, conversational, slightly confessional. Not polished, not scripted-sounding |
| Speech pacing | 2.5–3.0 words/second. Natural pauses between beats. No breathless rushing |
| Voice backend | Single TTS voice throughout the entire video — do not mix backends or voices between scenes |
| Persona generation | Single headshot/medium-shot image; establish visual description lock after generation; all talking-head director scenes reference that locked description verbatim |
| Sample phrasing rhythm | Short declarative sentences. "It was just — a lot." "I didn't know where to start." "They made it so simple." |

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**Type:** Spoken peer recommendation + on-screen URL

**Structure:**
1. Spoken: acknowledge the viewer's situation directly ("If you're dealing with something similar…")
2. Spoken: name the action and the URL ("just go to [url] — they'll give you a fair offer")
3. Spoken: warm, low-pressure close ("No pressure, no obligation — it was worth it for us")
4. On-screen: URL in readable text, lower-center
5. On-screen: CTA pill ("GET YOUR [OFFER]" or equivalent), bottom-center, brand color, fade in at 2 s

**Hard rules:**
- CTA overlay appears on the CTA scene only — not on earlier scenes
- URL must be legible at phone size (not too small, not obscured by caption text)
- No hard-sell language ("ACT NOW", "LIMITED TIME") — peer-advice register only
- CTA pill text should name the specific action, not just "Learn More"

---

## 7. Compliance Notes (Housing / Financial Ads)

When the product touches housing, credit, employment, or financial services:

- **No identity language.** Never reference race, national origin, religion, sex, family status, disability. Frame everything around the property situation or financial circumstance only.
- **Situation framing only.** "inherited home," "out-of-state property," "deferred maintenance" — not demographic descriptors.
- **Meta Special Ad Category:** Set `HOUSING` (or `CREDIT` / `EMPLOYMENT` as applicable) at the campaign level. Do not attempt audience narrowing by ZIP code or demographic.

---

## 8. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

These are locked-in corrections for this pattern. Treat each as non-negotiable in any future run.

1. **Single voice backend throughout.** All scenes use the same TTS voice. Do not mix TTS providers across scenes of the same video.
2. **No background music.** The UGC authenticity depends on silence. Do not add a music layer even if the platform "usually" has music.
3. **Verify words-per-second before director dispatch.** Each talking-head scene must hit 2.5–3.0 w/s for its target duration. Over-long copy causes rushed speech; fix copy, not duration.
4. **Visual description lock.** After generating the persona image, extract a precise visual description and embed it verbatim in every subsequent director call that features the speaker. Do not paraphrase or shorten across scenes.
5. **Brand watermark on all talking-head scenes, not b-roll.** The watermark pill appears on Scenes 1, 2, 4, 5. Not on the silent b-roll cutaway.
6. **CTA overlay on CTA scene only.** CTA pill + URL overlay is Scene 5 (CTA) only. Earlier scenes are caption-only.
7. **B-roll is a separate generated asset.** Generate the house/object exterior image independently (not from persona pipeline), then reference it as a PRODUCT REFERENCE in the b-roll director scene.
8. **No ring light, no bokeh.** Both are instant "ad" tells that break UGC trust. The lighting spec is natural window light, one-sided.
9. **Housing compliance framing.** No identity language in script or targeting. Situation-only framing.
10. **Caption preset:** outlined-text, Montserrat, word-by-word. Do not substitute a different preset.
