# Skeptic Hook Library

Drop-in opening lines for the skeptic-pivot pattern. All trail off (ellipsis), all aim doubt at the category, all ≤8 words. These are generic category templates — swap in your own product.

## Oral care
- "Mouthwash that actually works? I doubted it…"
- "Figured whitening strips were a scam…"
- "I didn't believe a toothpaste could fix this…"
- "Charcoal whatever — I was over it…"

## Skincare
- "I thought retinol just burned your face…"
- "I gave up on serums a year ago…"
- "Another vitamin C? I almost skipped…"
- "I figured my skin was just like this…"

## Haircare / scalp
- "I figured nothing was gonna grow this back…"
- "I'd tried every shampoo. Nothing…"
- "I assumed thinning was just it…"

## Supplements / wellness
- "I thought sleep gummies were placebo…"
- "Greens powders all taste like grass — I was done…"
- "Magnesium TikTok was overhyped, I figured…"
- "Another nootropic. Sure…"

## Body care
- "I thought deodorant was deodorant…"
- "I figured stretch marks were forever…"

## Anti-patterns (do not use)
- ❌ "This [Brand] product changed my life!" — hype, not doubt
- ❌ "POV: you discover the best serum" — POV framing dilutes the pivot
- ❌ "Three reasons I love this…" — listicle framing, wrong mode
- ❌ "I was today years old when I learned…" — overused, no skeptic energy
