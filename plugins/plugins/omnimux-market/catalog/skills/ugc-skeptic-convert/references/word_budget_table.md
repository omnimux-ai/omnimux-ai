# Word Budget Table

Target pacing: **2.5 words/second**. Hard ceiling: **3.0 w/s** (above this, VO sounds rushed and unnatural).

| Runtime | Target words (2.5 w/s) | Ceiling (3.0 w/s) | Notes |
|---------|------------------------|-------------------|-------|
| 8s  | 20 | 24 | Tight. Drop the reason beat to a single sensory word. |
| 10s | 25 | 30 | Sweet spot for a 3-beat arc. |
| 12s | 30 | 36 | Comfortable. Room for a real reason beat. |
| 15s | 37 | 45 | Pattern starts to drag past 12s — only stretch if the reason genuinely needs it. |

## Beat-level allocation (12s reference)

Generic example lines — rewrite for your product, keep the word counts.

| Beat | Words | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| Doubt | 5–7 | "I thought this stuff was BS…" |
| Pivot | 4–6 | "…tried it for a week." |
| Reason | 10–14 | "No fuss. It actually does what it says." |
| Close | 4–6 | "Yeah. I get it now." |

## Trimming order (when over budget)

1. **Adjectives first.** "Refreshingly clean minty taste" → "actually fresh"
2. **Filler phrases.** "I have to say", "honestly speaking", "to be fair" — these eat 3–5 words and contribute nothing
3. **The reason beat.** Cut to one concrete observation. One.
4. **Last resort: shorten the hook.** Only if you've cut everything else. The hook is the most load-bearing line.

## Do not cut

- The trail-off after the hook (the audible pause IS the pivot setup)
- The close (a 12s ad with no soft sign-off feels truncated)
