# UGC Curiosity Hook → App Reveal Pattern

A 30-second talking-head creative structure that turns a mobile app into a "why didn't I know about this sooner?" discovery moment. Built for TikTok 9:16 vertical video; maps directly to Instagram Reels. The creative DNA is the conspiratorial lean-in + deadpan problem + punchy one-word reveal + warm close.

---

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Strong fit:**
- Mobile utility apps with an obvious, underserved pain point (habit tracking, language learning, receipt scanning, sleep tracking, etc.)
- Gen Z / millennial audiences on TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts
- Free app with App Store or Play Store CTA
- Brand wants organic/authentic UGC feel, not polished brand advertising

**Poor fit:**
- B2B SaaS or enterprise products
- Products requiring complex explanation (> 1 feature can't be conveyed in a gesture)
- Luxury goods where conspiratorial tone undermines premium positioning
- Audiences 35+ where UGC authenticity signals may read as low-production

---

## 2. Hook & Opening (0–5 s)

**Structural move: Conspiratorial Curiosity Question**

The creator leans slightly toward camera, lowers voice a touch, and poses a rhetorical "why does no one talk about X?" question. This is a *pattern interrupt* that makes the viewer feel like they're about to receive insider information. It is NOT a question the viewer answers; it's a rhetorical frame that positions the product as an under-the-radar gem.

**Generic example:**
> *"Why does no one talk about this app?"* — delivered with a slight conspiratorial lean toward camera, hushed but clear.

**Swap rule:** Replace "this app" with a category descriptor if desired (e.g., "this scanner app", "this sleep tracker"), but keep the "why does no one talk about…" frame intact. The lean-in body language is non-negotiable — it signals intimacy and sets the conspiratorial tone for the whole ad.

**What's locked:** The rhetorical question structure, the lean-in, the hushed-but-direct delivery.
**What's swappable:** The specific product category noun, the creator persona.

---

## 3. Narrative Arc

| Beat | Window | Voice/Visual Tone | Purpose |
|------|--------|-------------------|---------|
| **HOOK** | 0–5 s | Conspiratorial, hushed, direct eye contact, slight lean in | Create curiosity; make viewer feel they're about to hear a secret |
| **PROBLEM** | 5–13 s | Deadpan, relatable, gesture-driven mime | Validate the pain point; viewer nods "yeah, that's me" |
| **SOLUTION** | 13–22 s | Building energy, lifts phone showing app, punchy resolution word | Name the product; show it works; land the payoff word ("Instantly.") |
| **CLOSER** | 22–30 s | Warm, light laugh, soft sign-off, spoken CTA | Build trust; make CTA feel friendly not salesy |

**Arc name:** *Discovery-Agitation-Reveal-Warmth*. It's not a hard sell arc; it's a "friend telling you about something cool" arc. The energy builds gently through beats 1–3, then releases warmly in beat 4. Do not inject hype or urgency language — it breaks the conspiratorial/authentic register.

**Pacing fingerprint:**
- Beat 1: ~1.6 words/second (slow, deliberate)
- Beat 2: ~3.0 words/second (energized, relatable riff)
- Beat 3: ~2.1 words/second (building, then hard stop at the payoff word)
- Beat 4: ~2.4 words/second (conversational, warm)

The *fastest cut in speech rate* happens in Beat 2 (the agitation riff). Beat 3 then pulls back to let the payoff word land in silence.

---

## 4. Visual Style Spec

**Format:** UGC talking-head. The creator is the only visual subject until the phone-hold reveal.

**Setting:** Cozy, lived-in indoor environment — bedroom, living room, kitchen nook. Slightly cluttered is fine. Avoid sterile white backgrounds or clearly branded studio setups.

**Camera grammar:**
- Locked-off or very slight handheld wobble (not stabilized-smooth; not shaky-vlog)
- Medium close-up to close-up on creator's face throughout
- No cuts between creator shots in beats 1–2; hold on the face
- Beat 3: hold up phone toward camera so the app UI / icon is legible

**Color palette:** Naturalistic, no heavy color grading. Warm-neutral ambient (bedroom lamps, window light). The app's brand accent color is reserved for caption overlays only — do not color-grade the entire frame to match the brand.

**On-screen text:**
- Word-by-word caption preset (outlined-text / Poppins or equivalent legible rounded sans-serif)
- Captions at lower-third position; do not obscure face
- Beat 4 CTA: App name in the brand accent color (Anton or bold display font), tagline in white DM Sans or equivalent clean sans. Fade in with the spoken CTA, hold through end of video.
- No other text overlays; let the spoken word carry the content

**Shot length distribution:** Long-ish holds (4–9 s per scene); this is NOT a fast-cut TikTok. The conspiratorial intimacy requires the viewer to sit with the creator's face.

---

## 5. Voice & Persona

**Tone:** Deadpan-conspiratorial in beat 1 → relatable-energized in beat 2 → punchy-confident in beat 3 → warm-friendly in beat 4.

**Persona archetype:** *The In-the-Know Peer* — not an expert, not a celebrity, not a screaming hype creator. This is the friend who always finds the best apps before everyone else. Credibility comes from casual confidence, not credentials.

**Demographic anchor (swappable):** Early 20s, Gen Z or young millennial, casual clothing (sweatshirt/jeans), cozy indoor setting. The specific ethnicity, gender, and styling should match the target audience — all are fully swappable as long as the persona archetype and tone are preserved.

**Sample phrasing rhythms to preserve:**
- Beat 1 opener: "Why does no one talk about [X]?" — rhetorical, not a real question
- Beat 2 agitation: Short declarative sentences + a gesture + a deadpan confirming line ("Yeah. That." / "Every time." / "Same.")
- Beat 3 payoff: Name the app → one-sentence mechanic → single punchy adverb or adjective on its own ("Instantly." / "Done." / "Every time.")
- Beat 4 close: Light laugh or warm smile → social proof in one sentence → "It's free" (or equivalent value signal) → spoken App Store / Play Store CTA

**Speech pacing:** Unhurried. No filler-word anxiety. Comfortable pauses between beats.

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**Type:** Soft spoken close + on-screen branded card. No aggressive urgency language.

**Structure:**
1. Spoken: "[App name]. Free on the App Store." (or Play Store / both)
2. On-screen: App name in the brand accent color (bold display font, e.g. Anton) fades in at ~22 s
3. On-screen: "Free on the App Store" in white clean sans fades in just below, ~1 s after app name
4. Both cards hold through the end of the video (do not fade out before cut)

**What's locked:** The "it's free" value signal in the spoken close. Omitting this tends to reduce install intent for free apps — always include it even if brief.
**What's swappable:** Platform name (App Store → Play Store → both), exact tagline wording, brand accent color.

---

## 7. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

These are the non-negotiable elements of this pattern. Every future execution must comply:

1. **Conspiratorial lean-in on the hook.** The creator's body language must physically signal intimacy — slight forward lean, lowered voice, eye contact. A creator standing straight and speaking at normal volume breaks the pattern.
2. **Deadpan beat 2 with a confirming one-liner.** The agitation beat ends with a short confirming line ("Yeah. That.") — this lands the relatable pain before the product pivot. Do not skip this.
3. **Phone-hold reveal in beat 3 with visible app UI or icon.** The creator must hold up the phone toward camera. App icon / UI must be legible. This is the product proof moment. Do not substitute a screen-recording insert or a cut-away; the UGC authenticity requires the creator's hand holding the real phone.
4. **Single punchy payoff word at the end of beat 3.** One word, short sentence. "Instantly." "Done." "Just like that." Spoken with confidence, slight pause before it. Do not dilute with additional explanation sentences.
5. **Warm close, not hype close.** Beat 4 must feel like a friend's recommendation, not an infomercial. No "Don't miss out!", no countdown timers, no "limited time" language.
6. **Music at low volume (≤ 0.15 mix ratio).** Lo-fi indie pop or similar non-distracting instrumental. The voice is the primary audio element. Music must not compete with speech.
7. **Word-by-word captions always on.** This is a TikTok/Reels primary format — a significant percentage of viewers watch muted. Captions are required, not optional.
8. **No jump-cuts within a beat.** Each beat is a single continuous take. Jump-cutting within beat 1 (the hook) destroys the conspiratorial intimacy. If a re-take is needed, use a clean full cut between beats, not a jump-cut.
9. **Brand accent color only in CTA overlay.** The accent color appears exclusively in the on-screen CTA text. It must not be used for caption text throughout the video — that reads as branded-advertisement, not UGC.
10. **Product reference image as phone-screen prop.** When an app icon or product reference image is available, pass it as the product reference for the beat 3 director call so the phone screen shows the real brand asset, not a placeholder.
