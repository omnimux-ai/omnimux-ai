# Cinematic Dual-Persona Dark Luxury Ad

A 30-second, three-act, no-VO cinematic ad pattern for premium athletic and streetwear brands. All emotion is carried by visual momentum, dark neon cinematography, pounding instrumental music, and punchy kinetic text. Two hero personas (one male, one female) establish aspirational range without a spoken word.

---

## 1. When to Use This Pattern

**Best fit:**
- Premium athletic, streetwear, sneaker, or activewear brands
- Products with bold graphics, strong silhouettes, or distinctive color accents
- Briefs asking for "cinematic," "dark luxury," "hypebeast," "blockbuster," or "power"
- Platforms: TikTok (9:16), Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- Dual-audience products (male + female market simultaneously)

**Do not use for:**
- Soft, pastel, or wellness-first brands (use a warmer UGC or ASMR pattern instead)
- Briefs requiring on-camera testimonial, talking-head, or voiceover
- Single-persona mandates (adapt to solo if forced, but the dual structure is a core strength)
- Long-form (60s+) content — the pacing gets exhausting beyond 35s

---

## 2. Hook & Opening (Seconds 0–10): The Silent Hero Walk

**Structural move:** *Slow-motion aspirational entrance — subject owns the frame before a single word appears.*

The ad opens in silence (music only, no VO). A single persona moves through a dramatic urban nightscape — slow-motion hero walk on a wet neon-lit city street. No dialogue. No product call-out. Just the silhouette, the drip, and the atmosphere. Text is withheld for the first several seconds to let the visual breathe.

**Example beat (generic placeholder):**  
*A male persona (athletic build, buzz cut, full branded apparel kit) slow-motion walk on a wet urban street, anamorphic lens flares crossing behind him, atmospheric smoke low on the ground, dark-red neon key light, cold silver fill.*

**Swap the subject, keep the move:**  
Replace the persona and apparel kit with your new persona and product. The wet street, slow-mo, anamorphic flares, and smoke stay. The first text overlay should be a two-word or three-word power-fragment that names a quality of the wearer, not a product feature.

**Hook text rule:** First overlay = wearer identity claim (e.g. a short 2–3 word aspirational fragment). Never lead with brand name or product name — those land in the close.

## 3. Narrative Arc — Beat Sheet

| Beat | Seconds | Visual Action | Text Overlay | Tone |
|------|---------|---------------|-------------|------|
| **1 — Hero entrance** | 0–10 | Solo persona, slow-mo urban walk, full atmosphere | Short power-fragment identity claim | Dark, magnetic, breathless |
| **2 — Multi-cut kinetic burst** | 10–20 | Rapid-fire cuts: wide action shot → fabric/product ECU → second persona action mid-shot. Hard cuts, mixed distances. | Two short overlays: capability claim + power statement | Explosive, high-energy, visceral |
| **3 — Dual hero money shot** | 20–30 | Both personas in frame, sweeping silver light beams, fog, power pose. Cinematic closer. | Brand name + brand tagline | Iconic, awe-inspiring, conclusive |

**Beat 2 is the engine.** It is where the product is seen in motion, where the second persona is introduced, and where the edit rhythm peaks. Minimum 3 cuts in 10 seconds. Include at least one extreme close-up of fabric, logo, or signature product detail.

**Arc name:** *"Silhouette → Explosion → Monument"*

---

## 4. Visual Style Spec

**Mode:** Cinematic (NOT UGC). Everything is composed, lit, and graded.

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Lens / DoF | 85mm equivalent, shallow depth-of-field, anamorphic widescreen stretch |
| Key light color | Dark red / crimson neon |
| Fill light color | Cold silver / steel blue |
| Background | Deep blacks — no blown highlights, no bright backgrounds |
| Atmosphere | Atmospheric smoke (low ground fog), rain reflections on wet pavement, visible lens flares crossing horizontally |
| Shot length | Beat 1: 2–4s holds; Beat 2: 1–2s cuts; Beat 3: 3–5s hold |
| Fastest cut | ≤1.5 seconds (Beat 2 ECU cuts) |
| Color grade | Deep blacks, crushed shadows, red channel pushed in midtones, desaturated except product accent colors |
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 vertical (1080×1920) |
| On-screen text | All-caps only, no lowercase, no sentence case |
| Caption preset | `shout-block` / Anton font — high-contrast, large, centered |
| Text shadow | Dark red glow (`text-shadow: 0 0 12px #8B0000`) |

**Typography stack (in order of hierarchy):**
1. **Anton** — hero headlines, maximum weight
2. **Archivo Black** — mid-ad supporting statements
3. **Oswald** — closing slogan pill / brand name lockup

**Atmosphere is non-negotiable.** Every scene must have at least one atmospheric element (smoke, rain reflection, or lens flare). Flat, clean, studio-looking scenes break the pattern.

---

## 5. Voice & Persona

**This pattern has no voiceover.** All voice is replaced by kinetic text and music.

**Music spec:**
- Genre: Dark cinematic trap / hip-hop instrumental
- No vocals — purely instrumental
- Bass-heavy, cinematic low-end dominant
- Tempo: mid-tempo with heavy downbeat accents (not fast EDM)
- Duration: match or slightly exceed video length (32s for a 30s video)
- Volume: `0.35` (higher than standard because there is no VO to duck under)

**Persona archetype:** *The Aspirational Peer* — not a celebrity, not a spokesperson. Personas look like highly athletic, market-relevant people who simply exist in this world. Match the persona demographics to the brand's target audience.

**Physical archetype (adaptable):**
- Male: athletic build, sharp haircut, intense neutral expression
- Female: athletic build, high ponytail or strong hair profile, athletic neutral expression
- Both: zero smiling in Beats 1–2; a slight confident lift in Beat 3 only

**No dialogue. No product explanation. No selling language in the visuals.** The clothes do the talking.

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**Pattern:** Brand name + tagline close, no explicit spoken CTA.

The final 3–5 seconds display the brand name in the largest typography of the video, followed immediately by a one-line tagline on a separate card or pill. No "link in bio," no "swipe up," no comment-bait. The CTA is purely aspirational — the product is the reward, and the brand name is the payoff.

**Closing lockup structure:**
```
"[BRAND NAME]"         [Anton, full-width]
"[ONE-LINE TAGLINE.]"  [Oswald, pill — end with a period for finality]
```

**Captions:** Disable captions (`mute_captions: true`) on the closing scene so the brand name card reads clean with no caption bar overlapping.

---

## 7. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

These are non-negotiable in future runs of this pattern:

1. **No voiceover.** Ever. This pattern is music + text only. Adding VO changes the entire DNA.
2. **Music volume = 0.35** (not the default 0.15). Without VO, the music needs room to breathe. Do not duck it.
3. **Beat 2 must contain an ECU of the product/fabric.** Wide shots alone are not enough — the product detail must be seen in at least one ≥0.5s extreme close-up.
4. **Both personas must appear in the final money shot (Beat 3).** The dual-hero structure is the core creative move; if only one persona appears in the close, the pattern is broken.
5. **Clothing IS the product — treat it as a hero asset.** When the product is apparel, pass product imagery as a persona asset so the clothing is rendered on-model, not as a separate product shot.
6. **[Hard cut] tags in multi-cut scenes.** Beat 2 cuts must be specified as hard cuts, not dissolves or cross-fades. Use `[Hard cut]` explicitly in director briefs for Beat 2.
7. **First text overlay is not the brand name.** Brand name appears only in Beat 3. Beats 1 and 2 use aspirational identity/capability statements.
8. **Mute captions on closing scene.** Set `mute_captions: true` for the Beat 3 scene to keep the brand card clean.
9. **Atmospheric elements required in every scene.** Smoke, rain reflections, and/or horizontal anamorphic lens flares must be present across all three beats.
10. **All text overlays = ALL CAPS.** No exceptions. No mixed case. No sentence case.

## Quick Reference — Text Overlay Templates

| Beat | Slot | Example | Constraint |
|------|------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | Identity claim | "RISE EVERY DAY." | 2–3 words, wearer-focused, not product feature |
| 2a | Capability | "OWN THE MOMENT." | Action verb + object, ≤4 words |
| 2b | Brand quality | "STRENGTH MEETS GRACE." | Product virtue pairing |
| 3a | Brand name | "[BRAND NAME]" | Brand name only, largest type |
| 3b | Tagline | "MOVE BEYOND." | End in period, ≤4 words |

Swap brand-specific text freely. Keep the structural position of each slot fixed.
