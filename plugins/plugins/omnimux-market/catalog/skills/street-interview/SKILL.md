# Street Interview

Use when producing street-interview / vox-pop / man-on-the-street style ads — a host approaches real strangers in public with a branded mic, asks a hook question, and edits the most surprising reactions into a fast-moving montage that climaxes in a product reveal or recommendation. Trigger for "street interview," "we asked X people," "blind test," "asking strangers," vox-pop, public reaction ads, or any ad whose persuasion comes from real-stranger reactions rather than a single creator's testimonial.
