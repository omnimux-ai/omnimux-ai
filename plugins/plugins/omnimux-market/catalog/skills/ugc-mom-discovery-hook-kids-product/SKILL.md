# UGC Mom-Discovery Hook — Kids Product Pattern

A short-form vertical video creative pattern for children's products sold on TikTok Shop or Instagram. The core move: a parent-perspective discovery hook drops the viewer instantly into "I found something great for my kid" mode, three rapid benefit beats carry the middle, and a TikTok-compliant shop CTA closes. Voice-only narrator (no face-cam required) keeps it fast to produce while retaining UGC warmth through real product lifestyle imagery and child-in-use visuals.

---

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Best fit:**
- Children's products: art kits, STEM toys, games, educational sets, craft supplies
- Ages 2–12 audience, sold to parents (primary buyer = mom 25–45)
- TikTok Shop or Instagram Reels placements where organic/UGC feel outperforms polished production
- Products that have lifestyle or child-in-use photography available (even marketplace listing images work)
- Brands that want to emphasize mess-free, educational, or developmental benefits

**Not a fit:**
- Luxury or premium-prestige positioning (the warm UGC tone undercuts exclusivity)
- B2B or adult products
- Campaigns requiring on-screen talent (this pattern is optimized for voice-over + product footage)
- Products with no lifestyle or child-in-use imagery (the visual authenticity depends on them)

---

## 2. Hook & Opening (0–5 s)

**Structural move: POV Discovery Question / Exclamation**

The hook positions the viewer as the parent discovering the product. It does NOT open with the product name or brand — it opens with the parent's emotional state.

**Invented generic example (for a neutral hypothetical product):**
> "POV: You finally found the one 🎁"

This lands as a pill-style text overlay at the top of frame (not center, not bottom — top, so it reads before the eye travels to the product). Simultaneously, the VO opens with the same discovery energy:
> "Okay parents, this is the one."

**Swap the subject, keep the move:**
- Replace the emoji and the specific product sensation with whatever fits the new product
- "POV: You finally found screen-free weekend activity 🎨" — art kit
- "POV: The toy that actually keeps them busy 🚀" — STEM toy
- "POV: Bath time just got fun again 🐸" — bath toy
- The VO opener must stay conversational and addressed directly to parents ("Okay moms", "Parents —", "If your kid is obsessed with X —")

**Visual move:** Handheld zoom-in on product box or product hero shot. Slight motion blur is fine — it adds to the organic feel. Do NOT open with a static locked-off product shot; the handheld discovery motion is part of the hook.

---

## 3. Narrative Arc

**Shape: Discovery → Rapid Benefit Parade → Proof → Shop**

| Beat | Timing | VO Tone | Visual |
|------|--------|---------|--------|
| Hook / POV drop | 0–5 s | Warm surprise, conspiratorial | Handheld zoom-in on product box |
| Benefit 1 (mess/ease) | 5–10 s | Relieved, practical | Kit contents pan or unboxing reveal |
| Benefit 2 (child engagement) | 10–15 s | Delighted, proud-parent energy | Lifestyle: child using the product |
| Benefit 3 (outcome/skill) | 15–20 s | Impressed, slightly awed | Real user output / completed result |
| CTA / Close | 20–30 s | Confident, warm sign-off | Product box or result reveal → hard cut to clean product shot |

**VO pacing target: 2.2–2.8 words/second across all scenes.** Never rush above 3 w/s — it kills the conversational feel. Never drop below 2 w/s — it drags.

**The three benefit beats should each solve a real parent objection:**
1. Mess / setup / complexity → "No glue, no mess" / "Everything included" / "Ready in seconds"
2. Engagement / kid experience → Is the child actually hooked? Show it, don't just claim it
3. Developmental / lasting value → Fine motor skills, creativity, screen-free time, award citations

---

## 4. Visual Style Spec

**Style:** UGC-adjacent — NOT cinematic. Think "a mom filming with her phone," not "brand campaign."

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Format | 9:16 vertical, 1080p |
| Camera grammar | Handheld on hook/benefit scenes; slight pan on contents reveal; macro close-up on completed result |
| Locked-off shots | CTA scene only — hard cut to clean product box, locked off |
| Color palette | Warm, bright, slightly saturated — matches the product's own palette. No desaturation, no muted tones |
| Shot length | 5 s per benefit scene; 10 s CTA scene. Never cut faster than 3 s per shot inside a scene |
| On-screen text | Pill-style overlays; soft-round font (e.g. a rounded sans). Word-by-word captions |
| Caption style | Soft pill, word-by-word, positioned mid-lower frame. Mute captions on CTA scene — let the CTA pills dominate |
| Hook overlay | Top-of-frame pill, contrasting accent color (match product palette) |
| Benefit callouts | Bounce-in pill overlays per scene, distinct accent color per benefit (e.g. purple, teal, yellow) |
| CTA pills | High-contrast: primary action in orange/brand color + secondary "Link in Bio" in white |
| Music | Whimsical instrumental, soft and non-distracting, ~0.12 volume. Must not compete with VO |

**Real user imagery is a multiplier.** If the product has real user photos (e.g., completed result, child playing), use them in Benefit 3 over any stock or AI-generated imagery. This is the single biggest authenticity signal.

## 5. Voice & Persona

**Persona archetype:** Relatable Mom Narrator — not an influencer, not a brand spokesperson. She sounds like a friend texting you about something she found.

**Tone:** Warm, slightly conspiratorial, practical. Zero corporate language. Zero superlatives unless they're verified (see Hard Rules).

**Pacing:** 2.2–2.8 w/s. Sentences are short. Fragments are fine ("No mess. Just magic."). 

**Invented sample phrasing rhythms (generic hypothetical product):**
- "Okay parents, this is the one." (hook opener — direct address + conviction)
- "No setup, no cleanup — just open, play, and watch them light up." (benefit — contrast structure, sensory verb trio)
- "Real wood. Real pieces. And it actually holds their attention." (feature + social proof marker)
- "Fine motor skills, creativity, screen-free time — this kit does it all." (stacked credential list + closer)
- "Tap the basket and grab yours — link in bio." (CTA — action verb + reinforcement)

**Swap rule:** Replace product-specific nouns and feature lists. Keep the sentence rhythm and conversational markers ("Okay moms," "just," "actually," "and it").

**If persona image generation fails:** Fall back to voice-only narrator immediately. Do not delay production. The VO + product lifestyle imagery carries full authenticity without a face-cam.

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**Mechanic: TikTok Shop "Tap the Basket" + "Link in Bio" dual close**

The CTA scene (final 10 s) is structurally different from the benefit scenes:
- Mute word-by-word captions (the CTA pills ARE the text)
- Two on-screen pill overlays stack: primary action (orange, e.g. "Shop Now — Tap the Basket!") above secondary ("Link in Bio" in white)
- VO speaks the CTA aloud: "Tap the basket and grab yours — link in bio."
- Visual: reveal of completed result / product result → hard cut to clean product box (locked-off)
- No fade-out — end on the product box clean

**Why dual close:** TikTok Shop buyers tap the basket; non-shop viewers need the bio link. Both actions must be spoken and shown simultaneously.

**Compliance note:** "Tap the basket" is TikTok Shop platform language — use it verbatim when the ad is TikTok Shop. For Instagram Reels, replace with "Tap the link in bio" or "Shop the link."

---

## 7. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

Treat each as immovable:

1. **No before/after imagery.** Do not use split-screen or comparative imagery that implies a "before" problem state. Compliance requirement — must be honored for any variant.

2. **No unsubstantiated superlatives.** Claims like "best," "only," "#1" are prohibited unless sourced. Only use verifiable claims: award names (e.g., "award-winning"), age ranges from packaging (e.g., "ages 4–9"), developmental claims that appear on product materials.

3. **VO pacing must stay 2.2–2.8 w/s.** Do not exceed 3 w/s even for short scenes. If the script is too long for the scene duration, cut words, not speed.

4. **Real user imagery goes in Benefit 3 / proof beat.** Do not bury it in a middle scene. It is the authenticity anchor for the back half of the video — it must appear before the CTA.

5. **CTA scene captions must be muted.** Word-by-word captions running over the CTA pill overlays create visual clutter and undermine click intent. Always `mute_captions=true` on the CTA scene.

6. **Music volume ≤ 0.15.** Whimsical/playful instrumental must not compete with the VO. Hard ceiling at 0.15; target 0.10–0.12.

7. **Hook text overlay must be top-of-frame.** Not center. Not bottom. Top position — it reads before the product enters focus, which is what creates the "POV" visual grammar.

8. **Persona image failure is not a blocker.** If image generation for a UGC persona fails, fall back to voice-only narrator immediately and proceed. Do not hold the production for persona imagery.
