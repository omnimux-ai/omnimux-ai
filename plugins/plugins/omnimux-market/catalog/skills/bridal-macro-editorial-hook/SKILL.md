# Bridal Macro Editorial Hook

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Best fit:**
- Artisan / handcraft fashion: embroidery, beadwork, zardosi, lace, hand-painted textiles, couture jewelry
- South Asian bridal (Aari, Zardosi, Banarasi, Kanjeevaram), Middle Eastern occasion wear, or any cultural occasion-wear category
- Brands where the craft *is* the product story — the detail visible at macro zoom is more compelling than any lifestyle shot
- Product-only hero images available (3–6 close-up shots); no model required or desired
- TikTok / Reels vertical 9:16, 25–35 second duration
- Premium / luxury positioning; DM-to-enquire or boutique purchase model

**Poor fit:**
- Mass-market fashion or fast fashion
- Products where the body/fit is the selling point (activewear, streetwear, swimwear)
- UGC testimonial or talking-head format briefs
- Products with no visual surface detail (plain garments, accessories that don't photograph well at macro)

---

## 2. Hook & Opening

**Structural move: Aspirational Identity Claim**  
A single line that names what the viewer *deserves* or *wants to become* — not what the product *is*. It positions the brand as the answer before the product is shown.

**Invented generic example (for a neutral occasion-wear product):**
> "Your Big Day Deserves This ✨"

**The move:** Second-person address ("Your…") + occasion noun + "deserves" / "is ready for" / "was made for" + demonstrative ("this"). The viewer self-selects in within 2 seconds.

**Swap rule:** Replace the occasion noun and category noun only. Keep second-person + "deserves" + demonstrative structure.

| Original element | Swappable with |
|---|---|
| "Big Day" | "Anniversary", "Reception", "Engagement" |
| "Deserves This" | "Was Made For This", "Is Ready For This" |
| ✨ emoji | 💎 🪡 🌸 — one emoji, culturally resonant |

**Overlay style:** Minimal. White or cream text, centered, no background box, vanish after 2 seconds. Do NOT use bold or high-contrast cards — this is a luxury hook, not a hype hook.

---

## 3. Narrative Arc

**Arc name: Craft Reveal → Emotion Peak → Brand Trust → DM Close**

Total target: 28–32 seconds, 4 scenes of 7–8 seconds each.

| Beat | Scene # | Seconds | Visual | VO tone | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Craft Reveal** | 1 | 0–8s | Extreme macro: the most intricate detail (crystals, thread play, bead clusters) | Warm, soft wonder — "Look at this" | Hook viewer; establish craft quality immediately |
| **Emotion Peak** | 2 | 8–16s | Macro on motif with symbolic charge (heart, paisley, floral centre) | Intimate, slightly personal — "this is what handmade means" | Create emotional connection; name the technique |
| **Range / Depth** | 3 | 16–24s | Multi-variant or contrasting colour shot — shows breadth of craft, not just one piece | Confident, slightly editorial — "and it comes in…" | Reduce FOMO; signal variety without listing SKUs |
| **Brand Trust + CTA** | 4 | 24–32s | Most complete/polished shot — pulls back slightly to show full embellishment zone | Calm authority — brand name, location, close | Establish credibility; deliver DM CTA |

**VO pacing:** 1.75–2.3 words per second. 14–17 words per 7–8 second scene. Never rush — silence between lines is acceptable and elegant.

---

## 4. Visual Style Spec

**Category:** Cinematic editorial macro. NOT UGC. NOT lifestyle. NOT talking head.

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 vertical (TikTok / Reels) |
| **Shot type** | Extreme macro close-up (fill the frame with embellishment detail); no wide or full-body |
| **Camera grammar** | Slow, gentle push-in or locked off. No handheld shake. No fast pans. |
| **Lighting** | Warm gold / amber — bridal, candlelit, luxury. Avoid cool/blue tones. |
| **Color palette** | Rich jewel tones in the product (gold, burgundy, purple, copper). Neutral or warm background (ivory, cream, dark velvet if needed). |
| **Shot length** | 7–8 seconds per scene. No cuts within a scene. Each scene is one uninterrupted macro reveal. |
| **On-screen text** | Scene 1: minimal hook overlay only (white, centered, 2s). Scenes 2–3: word-by-word caption subtitles of VO (elegant serif preset, e.g. "verana" / Alice font). Scene 4: mute captions; use brand name lower-third + DM CTA pill instead. |
| **Caption style** | Elegant serif. Word-by-word reveal. Subtle placement (lower third). Never full-sentence pop-in boxes. |

---

## 5. Voice & Persona

**Persona archetype: Narrator-only (no on-screen presence)**  
No face, no UGC, no talking head. Voice is the only human element.

| Attribute | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Gender / age** | Female, young adult (22–30) |
| **Tone** | Warm, calm, quietly proud. Not hype. Not salesy. The voice of someone who *knows* craft and is sharing a secret. |
| **Delivery** | Slow and deliberate. Slight pause between clauses. Treat it as poetry narration, not advertising read. |
| **Persona setup** | `voice_only=true` — no visual persona needed |

**Sample VO lines (invented, generic — structure to replicate; swap product nouns):**

- *Scene 1:* "Every crystal placed by hand. Every thread chosen with intention." *(14 words)*
- *Scene 2:* "This is hand embroidery — an art form older than trends." *(11 words — OK if shorter, let music breathe)*
- *Scene 3:* "From deep copper to golden ivory — every piece is one of a kind." *(15 words)*
- *Scene 4:* "[Brand name] — bridal couture crafted in [city]. DM us to book your design." *(brand name + location + CTA — fill with the brand's own details)*

**Phrase patterns to keep:**
- Anaphora: "Every [noun]. Every [noun]." — establishes craft ritual
- Age/heritage anchor: "an art form older than…" — builds prestige without claims
- "One of a kind" — individualises, justifies premium price
- Brand + location at end of scene 4 (builds trust; locality = authenticity for artisan brands)

---

## 6. Music

**Match to cultural context of the product.** For South Indian bridal embroidery: Carnatic-inspired instrumental — veena, tabla, gentle melodic line. No western pop, no EDM, no lo-fi beats.

**General rule:** Music genre should signal the *cultural occasion* the product serves, not a generic "luxury" playlist vibe.

| Product heritage | Music direction |
|---|---|
| South Indian bridal (Aari, Kanjeevaram) | Carnatic instrumental: veena, tabla, bansuri |
| North Indian / Mughal couture (Zardosi, Banarasi) | Hindustani classical or semi-classical: sitar, sarod, tabla |
| Middle Eastern (Abaya, Kaftan) | Oud + light percussion; Maqam-influenced |
| Western bridal couture / lace | Strings quartet, piano, ambient classical |

**Volume:** Low — 0.10–0.15. VO is primary; music is atmospheric texture, never competing.

## 7. CTA Mechanic

**Mechanic: Soft DM Close with Brand Identity Pill**

Scene 4 has no caption subtitles (`mute_captions: true`). Instead, use two on-screen text overlays:
1. **Brand name lower-third** — elegant, minimal, placed lower-left or centered lower. Serif font. Small.
2. **DM CTA pill** — a contained pill/badge shape with the action phrase. Centered or lower-center.

**Invented generic example:**
> CTA pill: "DM us to Book Your Design 💍"
> Brand lower-third: "[Brand name]"

**Swap rule:** Keep "DM us to [action verb] your [product noun]" structure. Keep one ring/sparkle/craft emoji. Never use "Link in bio" — DM is correct for artisan boutique brands that do bespoke/custom work.

**Why DM, not link:** For bespoke / custom artisan products, a DM conversation is the correct conversion step — it signals personalisation and filters for serious buyers. "Link in bio" implies stock e-commerce; "DM us" implies atelier.

---

## 8. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

These are constraints that must not be reversed:

1. **No model, no face, no lifestyle.** All scenes are product macro close-ups only. This is a deliberate creative choice — the craft speaks for itself.
2. **Culturally matched music.** The music must reflect the cultural heritage of the product category. Generic luxury music is wrong for South Indian bridal.
3. **Elegant serif captions, not bold pop-in.** The "verana" / Alice font choice is deliberate for luxury positioning. Do not substitute display, handwritten, or sans-serif styles unless the product brief explicitly calls for a different positioning.
4. **Scene 4 mutes caption subtitles.** The brand name + CTA get their own dedicated on-screen treatment — no VO captions competing with them on the closing scene.
5. **Hook overlay is minimal style.** No card, no background box, no high-contrast treatment on scene 1. Minimal white text only.
6. **VO pacing: 1.75–2.3 w/s strictly.** Rushing the narration destroys the luxury feel. If the copy is too long, cut words — do not increase speed.
7. **Warm gold lighting direction.** All director calls must specify warm/gold/amber lighting. Cool tones contradict the bridal occasion warmth.
8. **Product image as PRODUCT REFERENCE in every director call.** Each scene must visually match the actual product asset — no generative hallucination of product appearance.
