# Luxury Brand Photo Reel

A still-image slideshow formatted as a short-form social video. No voiceover, no motion graphics, no talking head — just curated photos, minimal editorial text, and a luxury instrumental underscore. The mold fits any premium product or brand; swap the product, keep the restraint.

---

## 1. When to Use This Pattern

**Best fit:**
- Premium or aspirational products: home fixtures, interior design, furniture, fashion, jewelry, automotive, architecture, hospitality
- Showroom reveals, new collection launches, product feature spotlights, event photo recaps
- Brands that want to look elevated rather than trendy
- LinkedIn (thought-leadership / brand presence), Instagram Feed/Reels, Facebook Page posts

**Poor fit:**
- Products requiring demo or motion to understand (software UX, food preparation, how-to tutorials)
- Brands explicitly going for raw/DIY/authentic UGC style
- Situations where fewer than 3 usable photos are available
- Platforms where sound-off is the default and text readability is the only hook (Twitter/X feed)

---

## 2. Hook & Opening

**Hook archetype:** Silent Brand Statement — open on the strongest single photo (the one that most immediately signals "premium brand") with a one-line brand tagline as a centered or left-aligned text overlay. No flashy cut, no countdown. Let the image and the arriving music do the work.

**Structural move:** Image lands → music fades in gently over the first 1–2 seconds → tagline appears (fade-in, not slide-in) → hold for ~5 seconds. This tells the viewer "this is intentional and confident" before they've read a single product word.

**Invented illustrative example (neutral hypothetical product):**
> Scene 1 image: showroom display of the brand's flagship collection
> hook_overlay text: `"CRAFTED. REFINED. ENDURING."` — minimal preset, centered, white Oswald font

**The rule:** Keep the opening line brand-level (not product-specific), three words or fewer per clause, no exclamation marks. The product names come in scenes 2–4. Scene 1 is purely identity.

---

## 3. Narrative Arc

| Beat | Scene # | Duration | Content | Tone |
|------|---------|----------|---------|------|
| Brand identity hook | 1 | 5–6 s | Best brand/showroom photo + brand tagline overlay | Calm, confident, no CTA yet |
| Product introduction | 2 | 5–6 s | Hero product photo + product collection name + 1–2 feature callouts | Editorial, factual |
| Feature depth | 3 | 5–6 s | Second product or detail shot + differentiated features (finish options, materials, tech) | Informational but still restrained |
| Social proof / breadth | 4 | 5–6 s | Range / lifestyle / showroom context shot + range statement or awards/press mention if available | Aspirational |
| CTA close | 5 | 5–6 s | Final strong image (showroom wide, collection lineup) + CTA pill with website or contact | Direct but soft — invite, don't push |

**Total duration target:** 25–30 seconds. At 5 images × 5–6 s, 28 s is the sweet spot. Do not exceed 35 s; luxury audiences lose interest fast.

**Rhythm change:** No rhythm change — maintain a steady, unhurried pace throughout. Resist the urge to speed up at the CTA. The slow, deliberate pace IS the brand signal.

---

## 4. Visual Style Spec

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Format | Vertical 9:16 (Reels/Stories/TikTok) OR square 1:1 (Feed). Default 9:16. |
| Scene type | Still image (no Ken Burns, no zoom unless platform tools support it natively) |
| Caption preset | `editorial-clean` |
| On-screen text style | Minimal overlays. No slide-in animations. Fade-in preferred. |
| Primary font | Oswald Bold — collection/product names, taglines |
| Secondary font | Alice — feature copy, finish options, supporting body text |
| CTA font | DM Sans — pill/button CTA text |
| Text color | White (`#FFFFFF`) as default; warm gold (`#F5E6C8`) as accent for product names and premium callouts |
| CTA pill style | Dark semi-transparent background (`rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`), rounded corners, DM Sans, white text |
| Captions | `mute_captions: true` on all scenes — no voiceover, no auto-generated captions |
| Music volume | 0.4–0.5 (present but never overpowering — this is a visual-primary reel) |

**What NOT to do visually:**
- No neon/bright-accent colors
- No full-screen text slides (text should always share the frame with an image)
- No lower-thirds ticker bars
- No stock photo substitutions — use only the brand's own uploaded images

---

## 5. Voice & Persona

There is no spoken voice in this pattern. The "persona" is the brand's editorial voice expressed through copy choices.

**Tone:** Calm-authoritative. Confident restraint. The copy does not sell — it *presents*.

**Copy writing rules:**
- Product names in title case, always
- Feature callouts: noun phrases, not sentences. E.g., "Hand-Finished Edges" not "Available with hand-finished edges."
- Avoid superlatives ("best", "amazing", "revolutionary") — let the image make that claim
- Finish/variant lists: pipe-separated on one line. E.g., `Chrome · Matte Black · Brushed Gold`
- CTA: soft invitation. `"Visit [website]"` or `"Explore the Collection"` — not `"Buy Now"` or `"Shop Today"`

**Invented illustrative lines (neutral hypothetical product — structurally reusable):**
- Hook: `"CRAFTED. REFINED. ENDURING."`
- Product intro: `"The Signature Collection — Where Form Meets Function"`
- Feature callout: `"Hand-Finished · Solid Hardware · Three Finish Options"`
- CTA: `"Discover the Full Collection · brandname.com"`

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**Type:** On-screen pill overlay on the final scene. No spoken CTA, no comment-bait, no "link in bio" text (that's handled in the post caption, not the video).

**Placement:** Lower third, horizontally centered, with adequate padding from frame edge.

**Copy formula:** `[Action verb] + [brand/collection noun] · [URL or contact]`
Examples:
- `"Explore the Collection · brandname.com"`
- `"Visit Our Showroom · brandname.com/contact"`
- `"Request a Consultation · brandname.com"`

**Duration:** CTA pill should appear for the full final scene (5–6 s), not a late reveal. The viewer needs reading time.

**Do NOT add:** QR codes (poor readability in Reels), countdown timers, swipe-up language.

---

## 7. Music Direction

**Genre:** Luxury editorial instrumental. Piano + light brass or strings. No beat drops, no EDM, no hip-hop, no vocals.

**Mood descriptor for music_generate:** Use language like: *"elegant luxury editorial, piano and light brass, cinematic, slow tempo, no vocals, sophisticated, aspirational"*

**Volume:** 0.4–0.5 out of 1.0. The music sets the room tone; it should not compete with the text reads.

**Key rule:** Music must be instrumental-only. Any hint of vocals (even distant or reverb-washed) undermines the premium positioning.

## 8. Platform Posting Tips (deliver with the final asset)

| Platform | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Instagram Reels** | 9:16 native. Post caption: include product names + CTA URL as text since links aren't clickable. Add 3–5 niche hashtags, not 30. |
| **Instagram Feed** | Square 1:1 crop preferred. Same caption strategy. |
| **LinkedIn** | Upload as native video (not YouTube link). Caption: lead with a professional insight ("Behind the design of our signature collection…") before the product pitch. Tag the brand page. |
| **Facebook** | Native upload. Add location tag if showroom-specific. Boost eligibility: no copyrighted music (use generated music only, which this skill does). |

---

## 9. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

Treat each as non-negotiable:

1. **No voiceover.** `mute_captions: true` on every scene. This reel is music + text only.
2. **Warm gold accent, not white-only.** `#F5E6C8` for product names and premium callouts. Pure white for body/CTA. Both together read as luxury — white-only reads as generic.
3. **Oswald + Alice + DM Sans font trio.** Do not substitute generic sans-serif for all layers. The contrast between Oswald's condensed boldness and Alice's editorial serif warmth is intentional.
4. **Music volume 0.4–0.5.** Never go above 0.6 — it starts to feel like a commercial jingle.
5. **CTA pill on final scene only.** Do not repeat the website URL as a text overlay on every scene. One clean close is the luxury move.
6. **Use only the user's uploaded photos.** Do not generate, source, or substitute stock images. If a scene has no suitable photo, skip it rather than filling with a stock shot.
7. **5–6 seconds per scene, 25–30 s total.** Do not rush to fit more content. If the user has 3 photos, make a 15–18 s reel — don't pad or repeat.
8. **Scene 1 is brand-level, not product-level.** The hook overlay should be a brand statement, not the first product name. Product names start at scene 2.
