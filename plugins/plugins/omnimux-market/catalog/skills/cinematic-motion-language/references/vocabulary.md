# Cinematic motion vocabulary

Load when a brief needs detailed language or a full scaffold.

## Camera contracts

- Static locked-off camera; zero pan, tilt, dolly, zoom, reframe, or shake.
- Slow push-in only; ten percent scale change over the full duration.
- One continuous handheld drift with slight organic sway and no cuts.

State only controls supported by the selected model.

## Motion anchors

Pair analogy and timing:

- dust suspended in honey; crosses one quarter of frame width in eight seconds;
- embers in still air; rise from lower-right to center over the full clip;
- one full revolution across ten seconds with no acceleration;
- fabric settles under gravity within two seconds, then remains still.

## Spatial zones

- left third: intentional black void; no light, particles, subject, or movement;
- center third: subject remains inside this region;
- foreground: particles may cross the lens plane but never obscure the face;
- background: fixed architecture, no morphing.

## Lens sequence

Describe focus as an event:

1. subject begins sharp;
2. a foreground element crosses the lens plane;
3. focus shifts to the element while the subject softens;
4. focus returns after the element exits;
5. state the repeat count, if any.

## Negative space

Treat empty areas as designed structure, not missing content. Name the region and prohibit spill, motion, particles, props, or reframing that would fill it.

## Worked pattern

Brief: a raised hand turns slowly through golden dust; left third remains black.

- Camera: static locked-off, no cuts or reframe.
- Motion: hand completes one smooth arc over ten seconds; particles rise like embers in still air.
- Focus: hand starts sharp; foreground particles cross; focus shifts to particles; focus returns once.
- Zones: all subject motion stays in the right two-thirds; left third remains empty and unlit.
- Lighting: one warm key from upper right, deep shadow, no ambient spill into the void.
- Negative constraints: camera movement, fast motion, acceleration, left-side particles or light, abrupt cuts, identity changes.
