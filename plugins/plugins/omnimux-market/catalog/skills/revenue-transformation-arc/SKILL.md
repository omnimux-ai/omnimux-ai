# Revenue-Transformation Arc

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Best fit:**
- Food-service businesses (pizzerias, burger joints, dark kitchens, franchise networks) with a verifiable revenue or order-volume uplift story.
- Local-service businesses (gyms, clinics, salons) where a before/after number can be stated with confidence.
- Any market where a direct, punchy results-first tone lands well.
- Meta Ads placements (feed, Reels, Stories) and YouTube pre-roll where the 6-second hook must earn the next 85 seconds.

**Poor fit:**
- B2B SaaS or products where exact revenue uplift cannot be claimed.
- Premium/luxury brands where dark-grid grit would undercut brand perception.
- Markets that require soft, aspirational storytelling over hard numbers.

---

## 2. Hook & Opening (0–6 s)

**Structural move: "Compressed Revenue Transformation."**
Show the before-state number (red, pain color) and the after-state number (neon green, money color) with a directional arrow or fast cut between them, plus a time period in white. The viewer must read the transformation in under 3 seconds before any narration begins.

**Invented generic example (swap in the client's real numbers):**
> `$8K` (red, large) `→` `$40K` (neon green, large) `in 90 days` (white, subtitle weight)

**Swap rule:** Replace the currency figures and time period with the new client's real numbers. Keep the color coding, the arrow or cut between states, and the sub-6-second window. **Never soften the numbers or make them approximate** — specificity is the credibility mechanism.

**Visual grammar for the hook frame:**
- Background: `#0d0d0d` dark charcoal with a subtle grunge/dust texture overlay (low-opacity noise).
- Grid: thin 1-px white lines at ~60 px spacing, ~8% opacity — gives depth without distraction.
- Before number: Anton or Bebas Neue condensed, ≥72 px, color `#FF3B3B` (impact red).
- After number: same typeface, same size, color `#39FF14` (neon green).
- Arrow or transition: SFX hit sync on the cut (whoosh + impact hit, 60–80 ms).
- Time period label: white, Bebas Neue, ≈36 px, center-bottom of frame.

## 3. Narrative Arc — 12-Block Template

Target total runtime: **80–95 seconds**. Block durations below are guidelines; compress or expand by ±1 s based on copy density.

| Block | Name | Target s | Voice Tone | Visual Mode | Color Key |
|-------|------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | **Hook** | 0–6 | None (title card) | Motion graphic | Red → Green |
| 2 | **Context** (who the brand is) | 6–14 | Calm-authoritative narrator | Lower-third + logo lockup | White, Yellow |
| 3 | **Problem** (pain state) | 14–24 | Rising tension | Full-screen caption barrage | Red |
| 4 | **Agitation** (why it persists) | 24–32 | Conspiratorial, leaning in | Talking head or annotation circles | White, Red |
| 5 | **Solution** (the brand's method) | 32–40 | Confident pivot | Motion graphic schema | Neon Green |
| 6 | **Pillar 1** (service pillar) | 40–48 | Steady pace, one breath per pillar | Icon + label card | Yellow |
| 7 | **Pillar 2** | 48–52 | Same | Same | Yellow |
| 8 | **Pillar 3** | 52–56 | Same | Same | Yellow |
| 9 | **Pillar 4** (optional) | 56–60 | Same | Same | Yellow |
| 10 | **Social Proof** (client result) | 60–72 | Warm, proud | Testimonial card or revenue stat | Neon Green |
| 11 | **Credibility** (scale/magnitude) | 72–80 | Bold, declarative | Full-screen number callout | Blue |
| 12 | **CTA** | 80–91 | Direct, slightly urgent | Comment-bait overlay + spoken line | White, Neon Green |

**Arc shape:** Starts with a visual punch (Hook), builds problem urgency (Context→Agitation), pivots sharply to solution (Solution), substantiates with pillars and proof (Pillars→Proof), closes with a scale anchor and a low-friction CTA (Credibility→CTA). This is **problem-agitation-solution-proof-scale-CTA**, compressed.

## 4. Visual Style Spec

| Attribute | Spec |
|-----------|------|
| Background | `#0d0d0d` charcoal; grunge/dust texture overlay at 10–15% opacity |
| Grid | 1-px white lines, 60 px spacing, 8% opacity |
| Primary typeface | Anton (impact words) / Bebas Neue Condensed (labels, captions) |
| Body caption typeface | Montserrat Bold or Roboto Condensed for sub-captions |
| Caption style | **Word-by-word kinetic captions** — each spoken word pops on screen 50–80 ms after utterance; white by default, swap color on impact words |
| Shot length | Average 3.3 s; hook shots ≤2 s; proof shots up to 5 s |
| Camera mode for talking head (if used) | Shoulder-height, slight push-in, handheld warmth; never fully locked-off |
| On-screen text policy | Every major claim must be both **spoken** and **shown** — no audio-only claims |
| Transitions | Hard cuts only; SFX hit on each cut in the first 24 s; softer cuts in Pillars section |

**Color syntax — lock this, do not deviate:**

| Color | Hex | Semantic role |
|-------|-----|---------------|
| Impact Red | `#FF3B3B` | Current pain, problem state, before-number |
| Neon Green | `#39FF14` | Money, results, after-number, CTA highlight |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Captions, neutral narration |
| Signal Yellow | `#FFD600` | Services, pillars, action items |
| Magnitude Blue | `#00AAFF` | Scale claims, impressive numbers (client count, years) |

## 5. Voice & Persona

**Archetype:** Calm-authoritative narrator. Not a hype-man — more like a confident consultant who has seen the numbers and is slightly impatient with businesses that haven't acted yet.

**Tone breakdown by block:**
- Hook: silent or single declarative title-card moment.
- Context / Solution: measured, deliberate, 1 clear idea per breath.
- Problem / Agitation: pace slightly faster, rhetorical questions ("But why does this keep happening?"), conspiratorial dip in volume.
- Pillars: crisp, staccato — one phrase per pillar, no connectives.
- Proof / CTA: warm but direct; pride in the result, urgency in the ask.

**Sample phrasing rhythms (invented, generic — swap in real specifics):**
- "We took [CLIENT] from [BEFORE] to [AFTER] in just [TIME]."
- "And that wasn't luck. It was method."
- "Paid traffic. Digital menu. Review management. Loyalty system." *(pillar staccato)*
- "Drop [TRIGGER_WORD] in the comments and we'll reach out."

**Pacing:** ~140–155 words per minute. Avoid filler words. Hard pauses (0.5 s) before and after every revenue figure.

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**Type:** Comment-bait (spoken + on-screen overlay).

**Mechanic:** Narrator instructs viewer to comment a single keyword (e.g., a one-word trigger like "READY" or "INFO") to trigger outreach. This is lower friction than "click the link" and feeds the algorithm engagement signal simultaneously.

**On-screen execution:** Neon green text box, keyword in all-caps, white arrow or hand emoji pointing to comment icon. Appear at 80 s, hold through end card.

**Spoken close:** "[SPOKEN_KEYWORD] in the comments and we'll get in touch with you." Follow with brand logo lockup + jingle sting (≤2 s).

**Do not use:** Link-in-bio CTA for this pattern — it breaks the comment-bait mechanic. If the platform prohibits comment-bait, fall back to spoken soft sign-off: "Tap the link in bio and talk to our team."

---

## 7. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

Treat each as a non-negotiable constraint:

1. **Exact background color is `#0d0d0d`, not black (`#000000`).** The charcoal warmth is intentional; pure black flattens the grid texture.
2. **Grid lines must be visible but subtle** — if they read as a dominant design element rather than depth cue, reduce opacity until they disappear at arm's length.
3. **Color syntax is semantic, not decorative.** Never use neon green for a pain/problem claim. Never use red for a positive result. Swapping colors breaks the viewer's learned decoding of the ad.
4. **The 6-second hook must be entirely visual** — no narration voiceover during the before/after card. The image must speak alone.
5. **Word-by-word captions are mandatory throughout.** Do not switch to sentence-level subtitles. The kinetic pop of each word is part of the energy signature.
6. **Revenue figures must be real and client-specific.** Never use round/placeholder numbers without confirmation from the client. Fabricated numbers void the credibility mechanic.
7. **Brand branding stays consistent:** logo lockup appears in Context block (≈8 s mark) and end card. Do not insert it mid-narrative.
8. **SFX hit sync on the hook cut is mandatory.** The whoosh+impact hit on the before → after transition is the moment viewers register the transformation — dropping it reduces hook retention.
9. **Talking head (if used) must be shoulder-framed** — not bust-crop, not full-body. The eye-line should be slightly above center frame.
10. **Pillar block must list ≥3 and ≤5 pillars.** Fewer feels thin; more causes cognitive overload. Each pillar is a single noun phrase, no more than 4 words.
