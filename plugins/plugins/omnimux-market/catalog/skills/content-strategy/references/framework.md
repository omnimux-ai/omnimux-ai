# Content strategy framework

Load when generating a scored content roadmap or topic cluster.

## Searchable and shareable

- **Searchable:** captures existing demand by matching a specific question and intent.
- **Shareable:** creates demand through original evidence, a useful tool, a strong story, or a defensible point of view.
- **Both:** answers active demand with an insight or artifact worth distributing.

Do not label an idea shareable without naming who will share it and through which channel.

## Buyer-stage map

| Stage | User job | Common evidence | Useful formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | understand a problem | broad questions, support themes | explainers, guides, original research |
| Consideration | compare approaches | alternatives, best, vs | comparisons, use cases, evaluation frameworks |
| Decision | reduce purchase risk | pricing, proof, objections | case studies, demos, ROI, reviews |
| Implementation | get value after choice | setup and workflow questions | templates, tutorials, examples |

Modifiers are clues, not proof of intent. Verify intent from actual results or customer evidence when available.

## Evidence sources

- customer calls and support tickets: questions, objections, exact language;
- surveys: repeated needs and requested formats;
- product/analytics data: adoption, retention, conversion, and failure points;
- keyword data: demand, difficulty, trend, and intent;
- current web/SERP research: competing answers, freshness, format, and gaps;
- sales/CS input: deal blockers, success stories, and implementation friction.

Respect source terms, privacy, and copyright. Collect only data the current task authorizes.

## Scoring

Use the user's scoring method when supplied. Otherwise this default heuristic scores 0–10 only when evidence exists:

| Factor | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Customer impact | 40% | How frequent, painful, valuable, and evidenced is the need? |
| Content-market fit | 30% | Can the business contribute credible, differentiated value? |
| Search potential | 20% | Is there verified demand or a defensible demand-creation path? |
| Resource feasibility | 10% | Can the team produce and maintain it well? |

Formula: total = impact × 0.40 + fit × 0.30 + search × 0.20 + feasibility × 0.10.

Use unknown, not a plausible number, when a factor lacks evidence.

## Pillar and cluster design

A pillar should align with a customer problem and product value, support several distinct subtopics, and have a realistic distribution path.

Pillar structure: awareness explainer → consideration comparison → decision proof → implementation guide.

Dedicated hub URLs are optional. A well-linked blog structure is enough unless the topic warrants a deeper resource architecture.

## Strategy document

1. Objective and constraints
2. Evidence, assumptions, and unknowns
3. Content pillars
4. Priority topics
5. Topic cluster and internal links
6. 30/60/90-day sequence
7. Distribution plan
8. Readback metrics and iteration rules

Useful readback signals include qualified search impressions, assisted conversions, saves/shares by target users, demo intent, sales reuse, onboarding completion, and content-assisted retention. Select only signals tied to the stated goal.
