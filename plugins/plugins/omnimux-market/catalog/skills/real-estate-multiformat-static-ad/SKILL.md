# Real Estate Multi-Format Static Ad Pattern

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Best fit:**
- Residential listings (condos, townhouses, entry-level homes) targeting first-time buyers, investors, or young couples
- Agents/teams with established brand colors who want cohesive creative across Facebook + Instagram
- Listings with a clear price hook (under-market, specific round number) or a standout feature (rentable suite, transit proximity, rooftop/deck)
- Campaigns going into Facebook/Instagram ad sets where multiple placements are needed simultaneously

**Not the right fit:**
- Luxury listings where aspirational video outperforms static
- Commercial or land listings with no lifestyle angle
- Agents without defined brand colors (requires a color system to work)

---

## 2. Hook & Opening

**Structural move: Price-Shock Question or Imperative Flip**

Each format gets its own hook targeted at a distinct audience persona. The hook is the headline — large, bold, white — placed prominently at the top or over the photo. Two proven archetypes:

### Hook Archetype A — Price-Shock Question
Leads with a price number that challenges the reader's assumption about what's possible.
> *"[City] Condo Under [Round Price]?"*
> *"Under [Round Price] in [City]?"*

Structure: `[City] [Product Type] [Price Anchor]?` — the question mark signals disbelief and stops the scroll.

### Hook Archetype B — Imperative Flip
Contrasts the reader's current state (renting, waiting) with the ownership outcome.
> *"[Current-State Command]. [Ownership Command]."* (two short opposing commands)
> *"[Use It]. [Earn From It]. [Net Benefit]."* (dual-benefit triple)

Structure: Two short declarative sentences, present tense, direct address. The second delivers the payoff — especially powerful when the listing has a dual-use angle (live-in + rental income).

**Swap rule:** Replace city, price, and product type. Keep the sentence structure and punctuation pattern exactly. Do not soften the hook with adjectives ("beautiful condo") — bluntness is the mechanism.

**One hook per format; each targets a different persona:**
| Format | Primary Audience | Hook Archetype |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| 1:1    | General / price-conscious | Price-Shock Question |
| 4:5    | First-time buyers | Imperative Flip |
| 9:16   | Investors / lifestyle dual-use | Imperative Flip (dual-benefit) |
| 1.91:1 | Retargeting / broad | Price-Shock Question (short form) |

## 3. Narrative Arc (Single Static Frame)

Each ad tells a micro-story in one frame. The reading order is the arc:

1. **Hook headline** (0.0–0.3s eye attention) — stops the scroll
2. **Price in gold** (0.3–0.5s) — validates the hook's claim
3. **Specs line** (0.5–0.8s) — beds / baths / sqft in one line, confirms it's real
4. **Feature callouts** (0.8–1.2s) — 2–3 icon + text pairs for standout features
5. **Agent attribution** (1.2–1.5s) — name, credentials, phone
6. **Brand sign-off** (1.5s+) — team name + tagline

Beat rule: Every beat must be present. Specs without a price = incomplete. Price without specs = untrustworthy. Agent without phone = missed lead.

## 4. Visual Style Spec

**Style:** Branded static — not UGC, not cinematic. Clean, professional, brokerage-quality. Photo is the hero; brand elements frame it without obscuring the property.

### Layout patterns by format

**1:1 (Feed square)**
- Full-bleed listing photo as background
- Navy gradient footer bar (~30% frame height) anchored to bottom
- Hook headline top-center or center over photo with text shadow for legibility
- All info elements in footer zone

**4:5 (Feed portrait)**
- Three-zone vertical layout: navy header band / full-width photo / navy footer band
- Hook in header band; specs + features + agent in footer band
- Photo zone ~55% of frame height
- Most text-legible format — use for maximum information density

**9:16 (Story / Reels cover)**
- Full-bleed photo (vertical/portrait shot preferred — balcony, interior, architectural)
- Dual gradient overlays: dark-to-transparent from top (hook text) + dark-to-transparent from bottom (specs/agent)
- Hook top-left or top-center; specs + agent bottom
- Feature callouts as short icon+text pills in mid-frame or within bottom gradient zone

**1.91:1 (Link preview / landscape banner)**
- Split-panel: brand-colored left panel (~40% width) / listing photo right panel (~60% width)
- Vertical accent divider (brand alert color, 4–6px) at the split
- All text in left panel: hook, price, specs, agent, tagline stacked vertically
- Photo right panel: no text overlays — let the property speak

### Color system
Adapt from the client's actual brand colors. The pattern requires:
- **Primary dark** (navy/dark): backgrounds, footer bars, header bands
- **Price accent** (gold/warm): price figure — the most important number, must pop
- **Secondary accent** (steel blue or light): secondary text, feature callouts, tagline
- **Alert/CTA color** (red or brand pop): divider lines, "$" symbol, accent elements
- **White**: hook headlines, spec text on dark backgrounds

### Typography hierarchy
1. Hook: Bold, largest, white — ~48–72px equivalent
2. Price: Bold, gold accent — ~36–48px
3. Specs (bd/ba/sqft): Medium, white or steel blue — ~18–24px
4. Feature callouts: Regular, steel blue or white — ~14–18px with icon
5. Agent name + phone: Regular, white — ~14–16px
6. Team name + tagline: Italic or light, steel blue — ~11–13px

### Photo selection rule
Assign photos to formats based on composition:
- Landscape/wide interior or exterior → 1:1, 4:5, 1.91:1
- Vertical/portrait interior, balcony, or tall architectural → 9:16
- If only landscape photos exist, use crop-safe center extraction for 9:16

### On-screen text policy
All text is baked into the image. Every text element must have sufficient contrast — dark shadow or dark background behind white text; never white text on light photo without a treatment.

## 5. Voice & Persona

**Tone:** Direct, credible, no hype. The copy trusts the numbers to do the work.

**Persona archetype:** Local Expert — authoritative but not pushy. "Here's the opportunity; here's who to call."

**Phrasing rules:**
- No exclamation marks on hook headlines — directness IS the energy
- No descriptive adjectives ("stunning," "gorgeous") — let the photo prove it
- Price always formatted with $ and commas: e.g. "$425,000" not "under 425k"
- Feature callouts as short noun phrases: e.g. "Rentable Suite", "Transit 2 Min", "Private Deck" — not sentences
- Agent credentials always included (the relevant licensed-realtor designation) — signals legitimacy

---

## 6. CTA Mechanic

**Format:** Agent phone number displayed prominently in the image — no spoken CTA (static ad).

**Placement:** Bottom of ad as part of the agent attribution line, clearly readable at thumb-scroll size.

**Mechanic:** Direct response — the phone number IS the CTA. No "link in bio," no "swipe up." The implicit message: *call the number you see.*

**Optional:** If the listing has a dedicated URL, add it as a small text line below the phone number. Keep it short (e.g., `brokerage-domain.com/listing-slug`).

---

## 7. Hard Rules / Do-Not-Regress

1. **All 4 formats in parallel.** Never produce just 1 or 2 formats. Dispatch all 4 image generation calls simultaneously — that's the value of the pattern.

2. **Gold for price, always.** The price figure must use the warm accent color. Never render it in white or navy — it must visually dominate the spec area.

3. **Each format gets a unique hook.** Do not reuse the same headline across formats. Each targets a distinct persona; recycling hooks collapses the campaign to a single message.

4. **Feature callouts are non-negotiable for rentable suites and exceptional transit access.** These carry investment/lifestyle proof and directly drive conversion. Never drop them.

5. **Agent credentials (the licensed-realtor designation) must appear with the agent name.** Required for professional legitimacy and REALTOR® compliance norms.

6. **No exclamation marks on hook headlines.** Bluntness is the mechanism. Exclamation marks dilute the effect.

7. **Photo composition drives format assignment.** Do not force a landscape photo into 9:16 without checking for a vertical alternative first.

8. **Split-panel with accent vertical divider on 1.91:1.** For brokerage brands with a navy/blue/red system, this is the signature layout element and should not be replaced with a gradient.
