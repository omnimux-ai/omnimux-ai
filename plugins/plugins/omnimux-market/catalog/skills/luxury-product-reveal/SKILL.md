# Cinematic Luxury Product Showcase

A no-persona, pure-product cinematic pattern for premium goods. The product is placed in a lush natural environment that mirrors its ingredient story; slow camera movement and golden-hour light do all the emotional work. No talking head, no UGC roughness, no kinetic captions — just the product, the world it belongs in, and a quiet brand close.

---

## 1. When to use this pattern

**Best fit:**
- Luxury or premium physical products: fragrances, perfume oils, skincare, candles, spirits, jewelry, watches, artisan food/drink
- Products with a natural ingredient story (botanicals, fruits, florals, minerals, spices, wood notes) that can be visualised in the environment
- Brands that want editorial / high-fashion positioning
- Platforms: TikTok (organic or paid), Instagram Reels, Amazon product video, brand website hero loop
- Vertical 9:16 format (1080x1920)

**Poor fit:**
- Products that need explanation or demonstration (software, appliances, complex services)
- Brands that want UGC authenticity or lo-fi relatability
- Situations where a human testimonial or talking-head is the primary trust signal
- Horizontal / landscape formats without re-planning shot composition

---

## 2. Hook & opening

**Structural move: Atmospheric immersion reveal**

The video opens *in* the environment before the product is fully visible — foliage, petals, water, stone — then the product emerges from that world. There is no verbal hook, no text interrupt, no jump cut. The hook is sensory: the viewer is pulled into a beautiful world and the product is already part of it.

**Generic example of the move:**
> Scene 1: Product nestled among blooms, golden hour push-in. The product sits among flowers; camera slowly pushes in from a wider environmental frame to a close-up of the product catching the light.

**Swap the subject, keep the move:**
Replace the flowers + bottle with whatever natural environment mirrors your product's ingredient story. A cedar-and-amber candle opens in a forest clearing at dusk. A sea-mineral skincare serum opens on wet coastal rocks. The structural move — environment first, product revealed within it — stays locked.

**Hook overlay (text):**
- Style: `minimal` — a single short line, light weight, positioned high (position_y_ratio approx 0.12), displayed for ~3-3.5 seconds then fading
- Content: a sensory or evocative phrase, not a product claim. E.g. a freshly invented generic line such as "Born from the quiet of nature" or "Where stillness becomes scent"
- Font: Alice (or equivalent editorial serif). Never bold, never all-caps, never kinetic animation.
- The hook overlay recedes — it should feel like a subtitle to the image, not a headline competing with it.

## 3. Narrative arc

**Arc name: Environment to Ingredients to Lifestyle Touch to Hero Reveal**

Four beats, ~5 seconds each, totalling ~20 seconds. Each beat has a distinct camera grammar and emotional register.

| Beat | Duration | What happens | Camera grammar | Emotional register |
|------|----------|--------------|----------------|--------------------|
| **1. Environment reveal** | ~5s | Product nestled in its natural world (flowers, foliage, water, stone). Camera pushes in slowly from environment to product. | Slow push-in, 85mm equivalent, shallow DoF, subject sharp, background bokeh | Wonder, arrival |
| **2. Ingredient world** | ~5s | Product surrounded by its key fragrance/ingredient notes (fruits, petals, herbs, spices). Camera orbits or arcs around the arrangement. | Slow orbit or arc, same shallow DoF, warm side-lighting to catch texture | Richness, abundance |
| **3. Lifestyle touch** | ~5s | A hand holds or cradles the product against a natural backdrop (sky, foliage, water). Prismatic light or lens flare catches the bottle. | Handheld but smooth, slight tilt or drift, golden backlight | Desire, intimacy |
| **4. Hero reveal + CTA** | ~5s | Product + packaging on a premium natural surface (mossy stone, marble, driftwood). Camera pulls back slowly to reveal the full composition. Brand name, product name, tagline overlays appear. | Slow pull-back, locked-off finish, anamorphic flare on cut | Prestige, closure |

**Voice:** None. No voiceover, no talking head. Music carries all emotional pacing.

**Pacing fingerprint:** No cuts within a scene — each scene is one continuous camera move. The only cuts are the four scene transitions, which are straight cuts (no dissolves, no wipes). Rhythm is slow and deliberate; the fastest moment is the scene-3 lifestyle shot, which can have slightly more energy (a gentle drift rather than a locked push).

## 4. Visual style spec

**Aesthetic:** Cinematic luxury fragrance commercial. Think high-end fragrance film, premium fragrance campaign, designer nature spots — not UGC, not lifestyle vlog.

**Style direction (use verbatim or adapt):**
> "Cinematic luxury fragrance commercial, 85mm shallow depth of field, warm golden hour sunlight filtering through tropical foliage, rich saturated colors — deep reds, golds, lush greens. Slow graceful camera movement. Anamorphic lens flares. Kodak Portra 400 color science. No text, no people."

**Adapt the environment clause** to match your product's ingredient story:
- Tropical / floral product: "warm golden hour sunlight filtering through tropical foliage, rich saturated colors — deep reds, golds, lush greens"
- Forest / woody product: "dappled forest light, deep greens and amber tones, morning mist"
- Coastal / aquatic product: "soft coastal light, sea-glass blues and sandy neutrals, gentle motion blur on water"
- Desert / spice product: "warm desert dusk, terracotta and gold palette, long shadows"

**Color palette:** Rich and saturated, not muted or desaturated. The product's own colors should be echoed in the environment (e.g. a red bottle means red and gold flowers in the scene).

**Camera grammar:**
- Lens: 85mm equivalent (tight, flattering, compresses background)
- Depth of field: shallow — product sharp, background painterly bokeh
- Movement: slow and intentional — push-in, orbit, pull-back, gentle drift. Never handheld shake, never fast pan.
- Flares: anamorphic lens flares on golden-hour light sources. Use sparingly — one per scene maximum.

**Shot length distribution:** Each scene is one continuous shot (~5s). No internal cuts. Total video: 4 shots x ~5s = ~20s.

**On-screen text policy:**
- Hook overlay: Beat 1 only, minimal style, serif font, ~3-3.5s, then gone
- Brand/product overlays: Beat 4 only (hero reveal scene)
  - Brand name: top-center, Alice font, gold color, small size
  - Product name: lower-center, same font family, slightly smaller
  - Tagline: bottom-center, lightest weight
- Caption preset: `verana` (Elegant Serif — luxury/fragrance). Never use bold, kinetic, or sans-serif caption presets.
- Beats 2 and 3: **no text at all**

**No people in the frame** except for the lifestyle beat (Beat 3), where only a hand/wrist is visible — never a face, never a full body.

## 5. Voice & persona

**Persona archetype:** None — narrator-only via music. There is no speaking voice.

**Music role:** Music is the emotional voice of the ad. It must:
- Start immediately (no silence at the top)
- Build gently across the four beats
- Reach its warmest/fullest moment at Beat 4 (hero reveal)
- Be instrumental only — no lyrics

**Music direction (default example for a luxury product):**
> "Orchestral strings and soft piano, understated luxury, instrumental, ~22 seconds"

**Adapt music direction** to product origin/character:
- French/European luxury: "Orchestral strings, Parisian elegance, instrumental, ~22 seconds"
- Japanese/minimalist: "Sparse koto and ambient pads, meditative, instrumental, ~22 seconds"
- Coastal/fresh: "Light acoustic guitar, ocean ambience, airy, instrumental, ~22 seconds"
- Dark/woody/masculine: "Low cello, deep brass, cinematic tension-and-release, instrumental, ~22 seconds"
- Warm/opulent: "Orchestral oud and strings, warm and opulent, instrumental, ~22 seconds"

**Music volume in mix:** 0.35 (present but not overwhelming — the visual is primary).

**Tone of any text copy:** Evocative and sensory, never functional. Avoid: "Buy now", "Limited time", "Click link". Prefer: ingredient poetry, place names, sensory adjectives, brand heritage phrases.

---

## 6. CTA mechanic

**Mechanic: Silent on-screen card close**

The CTA is delivered entirely through the Beat 4 text overlays — no spoken CTA, no comment-bait question, no "link in bio" verbal callout.

**Overlay structure for Beat 4:**
1. Brand name (top-center) — establishes authority
2. Product name (lower-center) — tells them what to search/find
3. Tagline (bottom-center) — the emotional close, not a directive

**Generic example of the structure (invented, hypothetical product):**
- Brand: the brand name
- Product: the product name
- Tagline: a sensory phrase echoing the ingredient story, e.g. a freshly invented generic line such as "Where stillness becomes scent"

**Why this works:** For luxury products, a hard CTA ("shop now!") breaks the spell. The silent card close lets the viewer sit in the feeling for a moment, then the brand name is the last thing they see — which is what they will search. The platform's own shop button or link-in-bio handles the conversion mechanic; the ad's job is desire, not direction.

**Swap rule:** Always include all three overlay layers in Beat 4. The tagline is the most swappable element — use the brand's own tagline if they have one, or write a sensory phrase that echoes the ingredient story.

---

## 7. Hard rules / do-not-regress

These are the non-negotiable constraints for this pattern. Treat each as a hard rule when applying this pattern to a new product.

1. **No people in the frame except a hand in Beat 3.** No faces, no full bodies, no lifestyle models. The product is the only protagonist.
2. **No text in Beats 2 or 3.** Text appears only in Beat 1 (hook overlay) and Beat 4 (brand/product/tagline). Mid-video text breaks the cinematic immersion.
3. **Alice font (or equivalent editorial serif) for all overlays.** Never use a sans-serif, display, or bold font for luxury product overlays. The font must feel like a magazine masthead, not a social media caption.
4. **Caption preset must be `verana` (Elegant Serif).** Do not use bold, kinetic, or UGC-style caption presets.
5. **Hook overlay style must be `minimal`.** The text recedes into the image; it does not compete with it.
6. **Music is instrumental only.** No lyrics, no vocals, no spoken word samples.
7. **Music volume: 0.35.** Do not raise above 0.4 — the visual texture is the primary sensory channel.
8. **Output size: 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical).** This pattern is designed for vertical-first platforms.
9. **Style direction must include "No text, no people."** This prevents the video generator from adding watermarks, captions, or incidental human figures.
10. **Product reference images must be used for every scene.** The product must be visually consistent across all four beats. Always pass the clean hero shot plus the most contextually relevant styled/lifestyle shot as product references per scene.
11. **Four scenes, ~5s each, straight cuts only.** Do not add dissolves, wipes, or transitions. The cut itself is the punctuation.
12. **Anamorphic lens flares are part of the style DNA.** Include "Anamorphic lens flares" in every scene's style direction. Do not omit.
